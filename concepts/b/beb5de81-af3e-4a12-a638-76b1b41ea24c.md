# Concept: SIZE
# Definition
QUANTITY of a DIMENSION.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The abstract notion of size is standardly expressed by dà xiǎo [[大小]].
2. Cháng duǎn [[長短]] refers to relative length.
