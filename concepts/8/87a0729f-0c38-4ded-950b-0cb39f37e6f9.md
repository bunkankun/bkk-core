# Concept: RELATION
# Definition
FEATURE of TWO OR MORE THINGS TOGETHER.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard way of speaking of a relation in classical Chinese is the idiom “X zhī yú [[之於]] Y”.
