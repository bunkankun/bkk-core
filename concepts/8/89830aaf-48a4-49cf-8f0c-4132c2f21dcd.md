# Concept: DIVORCE
# Definition
STOP one's MARRIAGE.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for divorce refers only to the husband sending away his wife: chū [[出]].
NB: Xiū [[休]] is post-Han, Tang.
