# Concept: HAPPEN
# Definition
ARISE as AN EVENT.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The only word that corresponds to a notion of an event occurring is yǒu [[有]].
2. Rán [[然]] can refer to something tending to happen in general.
