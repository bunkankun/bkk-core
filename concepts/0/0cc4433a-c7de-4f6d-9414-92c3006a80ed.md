# Concept: AMNESTY
# Definition
ACQUIT ALL of a certain CATEGORY of CRIMINALS by ONE ADMINISTRATIVE LEGAL ACTION.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
Sì [[肆]] is the ancient standard term for a general amnesty.
