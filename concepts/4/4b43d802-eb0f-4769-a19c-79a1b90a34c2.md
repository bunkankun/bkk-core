# Concept: ELEPHANT
# Definition
INTENSELY BIG BEAST which HAS IVORY TEETH AND A LONG TRUNK.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for the elephant is xiàng [[象]], and there are no common near-synonyms.
