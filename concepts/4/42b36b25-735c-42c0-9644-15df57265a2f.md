# Concept: ABANDON
# Definition
DISCARD SOMEONE WHOM ONE SHOULD CARE FOR.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
<div>1. The most general term for discarding something reluctantly, abandoning what one would have liked to keep, care for or continue to do, is qì [[棄]], which is more current under the concept DISCARD.</div><div>2. Shě [[捨]]/[[舍]] is less dramatic and less abrupt and can have abstract objects.</div>
