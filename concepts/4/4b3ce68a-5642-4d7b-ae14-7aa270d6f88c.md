# Concept: COMMERCIALISM
# Definition
DOCTRINE that SOCIETY SHOULD be CONTROLLED by MERCHANT INTERESTS OR GOVERNMENT SYSTEM in which SOCIETY IS CONTROLLED BY MERCHANT INTERESTS.
