# Concept: HUT
# Definition
SMALL DWELLING.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The general word for a hut is lú [[廬]].
2. Máo wū [[茅屋]] "thatched cottage" describes a typical countryside dwelling.
