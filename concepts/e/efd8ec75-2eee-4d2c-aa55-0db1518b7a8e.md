# Concept: HERD
# Definition
GROUP of COOPERATING ANIMALS.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
[[群]]
## Modern Chinese Criteria
牧群 refers to a herd of domestic animals.
獸群 refers to a herd of wild animals.
