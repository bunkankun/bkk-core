# Concept: MOTHER-IN-LAW
# Definition
MOTHER of HUSBAND OR WIFE.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for a mother-in-law is gū [[姑]], and this concept was of great importance in ritual practice.
