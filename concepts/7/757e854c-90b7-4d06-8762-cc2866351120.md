# Concept: ICE
# Definition
HARD FROZEN WATER.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The current word for ice is bīng [[冰]].
2. Shuāng [[霜]] refers to frost.
## Huang Jingui
HUANG JINGUI 2006
GEOGRAPHY 13. 冰，水在攝氏零度以下凝結成的固體，統稱。
凌，人工砍鑿的冰塊。
凍，厚冰。
澌，解凍時河中流動的冰塊。
