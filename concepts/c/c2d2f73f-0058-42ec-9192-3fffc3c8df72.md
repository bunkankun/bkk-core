# Concept: TONGUE
# Definition
SOFT ORGAN in the MOUTH BETWEEN the TEETH which is USED FOR TASTING FLAVOURS.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The current standard word for the tongue of humans or animals is shé [[舌]].
