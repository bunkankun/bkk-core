# Concept: EAR
# Definition
ORGAN of HEARING.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The current standard word for the ear is ěr [[耳]].
2. Guó [[聝]] is a rare word referring to the ear cut off dead soldiers in war.
