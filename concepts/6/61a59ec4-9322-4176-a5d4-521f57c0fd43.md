# Concept: COCK
# Definition
MALE CHICKEN
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for a cock is jī [[雞]], the same as for "chicken", but occasionally the expanded form xióng jī [[雄雞]] is used.
