# Concept: BABY
# Definition
INTENSELY YOUNG CHILD.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The current general word for a baby is yīng [[嬰]] sometimes expanded to yīng ér [[嬰兒]] "baby child".
