# Concept: LIGHTWEIGHT
# Definition
Of SMALL WEIGHT, OR EASY TO LIFT.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for something being of little weight is qīng [[輕]], and I have not found any current near-synonyms.
