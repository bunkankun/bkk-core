# Concept: FIRE
# Definition
HOT LIGHT which APPEARS ABOVE what BURNS.
# Criteria and general notes
## Old Chinese Criteria
1. The standard word for fire is huǒ [[火]].
2. Yán [[炎]] refers specifically to the flame.
