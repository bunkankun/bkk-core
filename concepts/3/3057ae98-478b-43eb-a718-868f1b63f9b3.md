# Concept: SOUP
# Definition
LIQUID FOOD that CAN be said to be EATEN AND NOT ONLY DRUNK.
# Criteria and general notes
## Huang Jingui
HUANG JINGUI 2006
FOOD 34. 羹湯。
羹，總稱。
臛，純肉羹。
臇，少汁肉羹，多作動詞，指烹煮少汁肉羹。大約元代起， “ 湯 ” 成為一類菜餚。
