- Super-entry: [也](../../super-entries/f/fb231638-744e-4d27-a316-38744e1d271e.md)

## Form
- Orth: [也](../../graphs/2/208b48d4-5b38-4edb-8418-80f4dcff11e3.md)
  - Pinyin: yě
  - Old Chinese: lalʔ
  - Middle Chinese: jɣɛ

## Definition
Yě 也 is frequently used at the end of sentences to form temporal subordinate clauses which provide background, often in conjunction with qí 其.
