# 傷: INJURE

- Super-entry: [傷](../../super-entries/d/d6b70f4e-b247-4e23-b70f-4eb2473e23eb.md)

## Form
- Orth: [傷](../../graphs/9/9beba073-10a1-4698-aa67-64ce7663fcdd.md)
  - Pinyin: shāng
  - Old Chinese: lʰaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɕi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
The clearly dominant general term referring to the inflicting of bodily harm is shāng 傷. (But note that the word also currently refers to mental harm and psychological distress.)
