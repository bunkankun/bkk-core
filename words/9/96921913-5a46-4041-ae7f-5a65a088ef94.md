  - Provenance: zh-x-mc resp=rec:baxter, zh-x-oc resp=rec:pan-wuyun

## Definition
Jìng 靜 (ant. zào 躁 "flurried), is a state of mind in which one refuses to be rushed into any action and is fully at ease.
