## Form
- Orth: [凶](../../graphs/c/cb916253-6535-458a-8849-c647416d87de.md)
  - Pinyin: xiōng
  - Old Chinese: qhoŋ
  - Middle Chinese: hi̯oŋ

## Definition
Xiōng 凶, shěng 眚 and jiù 咎 are archaic metaphysical terms for misfortunes.
