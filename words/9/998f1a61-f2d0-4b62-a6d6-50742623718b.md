  - Old Chinese: maŋs
  - Middle Chinese: mi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Wàng 望 (and the much rarer tiào 眺/覜 and zhān 瞻) refer to looking at something from a distance (often with connotations of admiration), and the connoted direction is always upwards.
