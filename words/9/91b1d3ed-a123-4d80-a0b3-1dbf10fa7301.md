## Form
- Orth: [止](../../graphs/6/6556964e-355c-4f58-93fa-31077a01ad93.md)
  - Pinyin: zhǐ
  - Old Chinese: kljɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tɕɨ

## Definition
Zhǐ 止 is inchoative and refers to the coming to stop so as to remain in a certain place after movement.
