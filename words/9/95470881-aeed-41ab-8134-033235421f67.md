# 宅: DWELLING

- Super-entry: [宅](../../super-entries/e/e9c4e2dc-3943-4941-84e2-dc39432941d4.md)
- Concept: [DWELLING](../../concepts/5/56bf35ea-dd91-4992-b06d-79f679208295.md)

## Form
- Orth: [宅](../../graphs/6/6d2f2b92-390a-4c62-9c6c-2b5cb4c9ddcb.md)
  - Pinyin: zhái
  - Old Chinese: brlaaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ɖɣɛk

## Definition
Zhái 宅 refers generally to the abode of anyone of any status, and the word is a polite designation for what is presumed to be a dwelling of some elegance and status.
