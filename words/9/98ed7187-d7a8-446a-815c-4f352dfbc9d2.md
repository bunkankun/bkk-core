## Definition
Nuò 諾 (ant. yǐ 已 "be unwilling") expresses a general agreement to do what is asked of one, and this is the most common word which can be used both by superiors and by inferiors when expressing agreement. [EXPLICIT], [FORMAL], [REACTIVE]
