# 決: DECIDE

- Super-entry: [決](../../super-entries/9/9f9e9b12-4840-40ff-9e9b-124840d0ffd4.md)

## Form
- Orth: [決](../../graphs/3/331f456a-f12b-4774-b87f-81762c7294d1.md)
  - Pinyin: jué
  - Old Chinese: kʷeed
  - Middle Chinese: ket

## Definition
Jué 決 focusses on the the result as such, and the word can be used about decisons already made where duàn 斷 seems excluded. (事已決 not: 事已斷矣)
