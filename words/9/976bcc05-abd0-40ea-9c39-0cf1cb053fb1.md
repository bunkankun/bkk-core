## Form
- Orth: [皂](../../graphs/c/c98aad93-3e5c-420a-928a-231133f5b9fa.md)
  - Pinyin: zào
  - Old Chinese: dzuuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dzɑu

## Definition
Zào 皂 (sù 素 "plain undyed white") is also very rare and refers to the appearance of coarse dyed black non-silken textiles, the original reference of the word being to the plant used to produce the black effect. [LITERAL], [PROSAIC]

## Senses
1. **[nadN](../../syntactic-functions/5/516d3836-3a0b-4fbc-b996-071cc48ba53d.md)** SHIJI: died black (of coarse, non silken clothes)
2. **[nm](../../syntactic-functions/e/e917a78b-5500-4276-a5fe-156b8bdecb7b.md)** black dye; mostly used in connection with clothing and other textiles; NB: the generalised meaning "black" is postHan (There are Sanguo examples of 皂白 )
