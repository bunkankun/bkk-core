  - Pinyin: jì
  - Old Chinese: kɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kɨ

## Definition
Jì 紀 is sometimes used interchangeably with jì 記, but the specific force of jì 紀 is to focus not just on recording but on the compilation and redaction of a comprehensive record.
