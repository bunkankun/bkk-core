- Orth: [通](../../graphs/0/0958ad9e-20d5-4ce4-9288-6c9417a52625.md)
  - Pinyin: tōng
  - Old Chinese: kh-looŋ
  - Middle Chinese: thuŋ

## Definition
Tōng 通 emphasises the absence of impediments or hindrances in the passage towards a certain place and it refers to the general ability to get through. [PROCESS]

1. **[vi](../../syntactic-functions/c/c20780b3-41f9-491b-bb61-a269c1c4b48f.md)** get through (of a way, as opposed to sè 塞 "be blocked"); **[4 Attributions](#75199d54-395b-433b-9923-c754107890bf)**

2. **[vt(oN)](../../syntactic-functions/e/e64a7a95-b54b-4c94-9d6d-f55dbf079701.md)** get through to a contextually determinate place
