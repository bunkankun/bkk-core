  - Pinyin: tǐng
  - Old Chinese: deeŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: deŋ

## Definition
Tǐng 艇 refers to the boat even smaller than the previous, with space for one or two people. According to HUAINANZI, this was originally used in the area of Sichuan.
