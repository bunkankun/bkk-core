  - Old Chinese: mɯl

## Definition
Wēi 微 refers to something being so subtle and only indirectly suggestive as to have no direct manifest reference to any concrete reality or meaning. [IMPLICIT]

## Senses
1. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** be subtle to the point where the concrete reference to reality or to one's meaning is absent
