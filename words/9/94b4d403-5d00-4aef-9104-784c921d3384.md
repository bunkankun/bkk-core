## Form
- Orth: [今](../../graphs/d/dfc93eb5-edb4-49b5-93e7-afe643a085de.md)
  - Pinyin: jīn
  - Old Chinese: krɯm
  - Middle Chinese: kim

## Definition
The current word for the present is jīn 今. Note 趙歧on Mencius: 孟子傷今時之君 "of his time, contemporary".
