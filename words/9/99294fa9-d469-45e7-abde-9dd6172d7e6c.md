## Form
- Orth: [由](../../graphs/0/067ccb92-367e-4550-b656-f8751cc3a917.md)
  - Pinyin: yóu
  - Old Chinese: liw
  - Middle Chinese: jɨu

## Definition
Yóu 由 "be because of" emphasises the "wherefrom" of something, is rare in early texts meaning "be because" but became very common at later stages of the language.
