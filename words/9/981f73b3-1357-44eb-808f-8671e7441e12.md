## Bibliography
- [ANDERL 2004C](../../bibliography/4/498d85c4-d730-4ae0-bcd2-e2f3a58da628.md) - Studies in the Language of Zu-tang Ji 祖堂集; page 622, fn. 1842; bi4ji4ng 畢竟 originated in the Tang period. According to SONG YINSHENG 1996: 160-161 and 201-202 the word has two meanings: one is 'finally; in the last analysis; in the end; etc.'. And in the other meaning the adverb adds emphasis to a question corresponding to Modern Chinese jiu1ji4ng 究竟 'actually; after all'. According to SONG there are 6 examples of the first meaning in ZTJ and six in BIANWEN, 14 examples of the second meaning in ZTJ and none in BIANWEN (see also CAO GUANGSHEN 1984: 116). The word survived in both meanings into later periods (e.g. JINGDECHUANDENGLU; HONGLOUMENG). In the course of time several forms of writing for bi4ji4ng became common: bi4ji4ng 必竟, zhi3ji4ng 止竟, zhi4ji4ng 至竟, bi4 畢, ji4ng 竟, zhi3 止, zhi4 至, etc.(see also ZHANG XIANG 1953).
