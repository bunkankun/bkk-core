  - Old Chinese: ɡ-raŋ
  - Middle Chinese: li̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Liáng 量 refers very often literally to measuring physical dimensions of any kind, but can also be used figuratively to mean "fathom, imagine fully the size of something". [LITERAL!]
