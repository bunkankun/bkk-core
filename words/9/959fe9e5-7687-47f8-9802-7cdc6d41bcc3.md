## Form
- Orth: [舸](../../graphs/e/eaa58fb1-5473-42cf-bdf6-2e70a2f87111.md)
  - Pinyin: gě
  - Old Chinese: klaalʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kɑ

## Definition
Gě 舸 refers generally to a large ship; according to FANG YAN, in Han times the word was used in an area on the middle reaches of Chang jiang.
