  - Pinyin: chěng
  - Old Chinese: phrleŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈhiɛŋ

## Definition
Chěng 騁 refers to speeding along in a disciplined fashion at maximum speed, but the word usually occurs in the current binome chí chěng 馳騁 "gallop along".
