# 善: EXCELLENT

- Super-entry: [善](../../super-entries/9/902621dd-4111-48d7-a621-dd411118d7f5.md)

## Form
- Orth: [善](../../graphs/9/9c10d3ad-bc3d-4cd2-b8c3-2c5452ed803a.md)
  - Pinyin: shàn
  - Old Chinese: ɡjenʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑiɛn

## Definition
Shàn 善 (ant. zhuó 拙 "inept"), when the word does not mean moral goodness, but is close in meaning to liáng 良, refers to an acquired specific skill or propensity for certain forms of action.
