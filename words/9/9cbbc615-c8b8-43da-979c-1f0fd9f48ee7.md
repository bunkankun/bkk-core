## Form
- Orth: [賤](../../graphs/7/7634c796-ddaf-4829-9672-1156dc423f5c.md)
  - Pinyin: jiàn
  - Old Chinese: dzens
  - Middle Chinese: dziɛn

## Definition
Jiàn 賤 (guì 貴 "hold in high esteem" and jìng 敬 "respect") are to regard someone as vulgar, and typically as of slight moral worth.
