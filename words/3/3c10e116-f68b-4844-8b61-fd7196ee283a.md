## Form
- Orth: [以](../../graphs/4/4a877402-3023-41b9-8e4b-e2d63ebfa81c.md)
  - Pinyin: yǐ
  - Old Chinese: k-lɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: jɨ

## Definition
Yǐ 以 refers to conformity construed as a matter of availing oneself of something as an instrument or guideline.
