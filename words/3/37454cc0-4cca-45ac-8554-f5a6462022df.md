## Form
- Orth: [子](../../graphs/0/07663ff4-7717-4a8f-a2d7-0c53aea2ca19.md)
  - Pinyin: zǐ
  - Old Chinese: sklɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tsɨ

## Definition
Zǐ 子 and fū zǐ 夫子 can be used to refer to the teacher within a given school by the adherents of that school.
