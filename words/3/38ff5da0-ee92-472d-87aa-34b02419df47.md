  - Pinyin: chì
  - Old Chinese: khrlɯɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈhɨk

## Definition
Chì 敕/飭 is an order from a person in an elevated position, and in post-Buddhist times chì 敕 came to refer standardly to imperial orders.
