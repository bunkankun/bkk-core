## Form
- Orth: [誡](../../graphs/9/90a379b7-2570-4130-8db8-6416a906f7f1.md)
  - Pinyin: jiè
  - Old Chinese: krɯɯɡs
  - Middle Chinese: kɣɛi

## Definition
Jiè 誡/戒 (ant. quàn 勸 "encourage") refers specifically to a warning against a certain explicitly specified course of action. The direction of this warning is downwards.
