  - Old Chinese: kluunʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kuo̝n

## Definition
Gǔn 袞 refers to the special official hat worn by the emperor, and the word is quite rare. In fact the word comes close to the meaning of "crown", except that no metal was involved.
