# 離: LEAVE

## Form
- Orth: [離](../../graphs/2/2d2f7b6c-dbf8-4377-b87a-e72d9fe6f64c.md)
  - Pinyin: lí
  - Old Chinese: b-rel
  - Middle Chinese: liɛ

## Definition
Lí 離 focusses on the distance established by leaving between a person and the place which he leaves.
