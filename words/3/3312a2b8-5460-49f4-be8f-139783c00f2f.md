## Form

  - Pinyin: fēng ní
  - Old Chinese: poŋ niil
  - Middle Chinese: pi̯oŋ nei

## Definition
Fēng ní 封泥 refers to the the piece of mud adhered on a document and impressed with a seal. The mud used for this purpose was worn in special tubes.
