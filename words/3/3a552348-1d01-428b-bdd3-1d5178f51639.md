## Senses
1. **[NPpr](../../syntactic-functions/c/c43c0bab-2810-42a4-a6be-e4641d9b6632.md)** BUDDH: Blind Dragon (King); skr. Mucilinda (the phonetic transliterations include Mùzhīlíntuólóngwáng 目支鄰陀龍王 and Mòzhēnlíntuó 目真鄰陀; this mythical dragon-king was supposed to reside in the state of Magadha in a lake near the Diamond-seat (金剛座). When Buddha reached enlightenment he is said to have been the first to pay reverence. He is already mentioned in the Han translation of the Fǎjìng jīng 法境經) **[1 Attributions](#4464764c-f717-4bb0-a040-b2a5b64f760d)**
