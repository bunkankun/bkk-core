- Orth: [契](../../graphs/b/b3a19c99-6fcb-4ea2-8cd1-7f779c397e21.md)
  - Pinyin: qì
  - Old Chinese: khees
  - Middle Chinese: khei

## Definition
Quàn 券 and the somewhat more archaic qì 契 refers to the physical object which constitutes the written version of a contract or an agreement.
