  - Pinyin: tíng
  - Old Chinese: deeŋ
  - Middle Chinese: deŋ

## Definition
The standard term for a courtyard is tíng 廷/庭. In Western Zhou and Chunqiu times important public prosecutions usually take place in tíng 廷/庭 belonging to a palace or (possibly) to a temple.
