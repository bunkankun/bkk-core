  - Pinyin: dé
  - Old Chinese: tɯɯɡ
  - Middle Chinese: tək

## Definition
There is no common word for a characteristic, feature, or property of something, but dé 德 and xiàng 象 are words that are used to refer to determining aspects of things. See NATURE
