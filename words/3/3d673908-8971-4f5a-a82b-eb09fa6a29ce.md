JZ 4.16: 借一算，步之，超一等 "One 'borrows' one counting rod and moves it leftwards (in Chinese terms: forward!), in each movement of this kind jumping across one column." [NB: this 等 comes from the Song edition, and in Daizhen edition the text is wèi 位. If Dai's reading is to be relied on, then this will mean that the meaning of wèi 位 "decimal position" is attested already in the Nine Chapters.]
