  - Middle Chinese: ji̯ɤ ɦɑŋ

## Definition
Yú huáng 余/ 艅皇/ 艎 was in Chunqiu times the name of the large battleship belonging to the kings of Wu. Later it came to be used as a general term for a large battleship.
