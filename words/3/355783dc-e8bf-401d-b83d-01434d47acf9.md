## Form
- Orth: [敵](../../graphs/e/e650efbd-2e76-4e82-812d-a9cbea2dbd6a.md)
  - Pinyin: dí
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-leeɡ
  - Middle Chinese: dek

## Definition
The general word for an enemy or opponent of any kind is dí 敵 (ant. yǒu 友 "friend") which can also often refer to mere competitors or adversaries.
