## Definition
Duó 度 is often more abstract in nuance and expresses something in the direction of a conclusive opinion about the matter assessed. Thus this meaning of duó 度 is close to the related meaning "reckon that, consider that". [FIGURATIVE]; [[RARE]], [[MARGINAL]]
