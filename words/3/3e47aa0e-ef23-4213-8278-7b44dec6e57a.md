## Senses
1. **[NP{PL}](../../syntactic-functions/3/37c81914-a5f2-4c6c-a69c-d61831609e97.md)** *[figurative](../../semantic-features/2/2e48851c-928e-40f0-ae0d-2bf3eafeaa17.md)* BUDDH: the Buddha-land of Permanence, Tranquility, and Light (the Pure Land (淨土) where the dharma-bodies (法身) of all Buddhas reside (in the Tiāntái School one of the four regions, 四土); the region is referred to as 'permanent' 常 since there is no birth, death, nor any form of change; 'tranquil' because all delusions and afflictions have vanished, 'light' because the wisdom of the Buddhas eminates radiant light **[1 Attributions](#5b11f9fa-7577-427c-acef-18a89c599dba)**
