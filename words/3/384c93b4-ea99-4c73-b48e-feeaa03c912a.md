  - Pinyin: huì
  - Old Chinese: hmɯɯs
  - Middle Chinese: huo̝i

## Definition
Huì 晦(ant. míng 明 "full morning, morning immediately after daybreak") and mù 暮 (ant. dàn 旦 "morning") refer to time when it is beginning to get dark. The focus is on reduced light conditions.
