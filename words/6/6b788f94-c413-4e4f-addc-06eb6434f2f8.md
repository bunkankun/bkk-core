  - Pinyin: pí bì
  - Old Chinese: bral beds
  - Middle Chinese: biɛ biɛi

## Definition
Pí bì 皮幣 refers to the money made of deer skin which were in use in the reign of Emperor Wudi (141 - 87 B.C.).
