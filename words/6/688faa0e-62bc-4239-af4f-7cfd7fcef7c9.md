# 卜: DIVINATION

- Super-entry: [卜](../../super-entries/0/0dac25b4-137b-4e6a-ac25-b4137b3e6a8c.md)
- Concept: [DIVINATION](../../concepts/7/75cc4c9e-41c1-4420-a0f7-3049e4ae97c9.md)

## Form
- Orth: [卜](../../graphs/f/f53f253b-d390-4e01-856a-d574e699966f.md)
  - Pinyin: bǔ
  - Old Chinese: pooɡ
  - Middle Chinese: puk

## Definition
Bǔ 卜 refers to the whole process of divination by oracle bone from turtle or bovine, including the heating of the bones, causing the cracks to appear, and sometimes even the incision of inscriptions on the cracked bone.
