  - Old Chinese: ŋɡlaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ŋɣɛk

## Definition
Nì 逆 is a dialect variant for yíng 迎 and is typically used for welcoming brides into a family, but in later times the word came to be used predominantly in the meaning "to oppose".
