# 閣: TABLE

- Super-entry: [閣](../../super-entries/3/3e5484d2-d09e-4f76-9484-d2d09e9f76f0.md)
- Concept: [TABLE](../../concepts/6/6138f0de-3527-4a80-8210-fdbdf169f7d0.md)

## Form
- Orth: [閣](../../graphs/8/81aaf8bb-9ae1-431d-a45e-532d6e864ac4.md)
  - Pinyin: gé
  - Old Chinese: klaaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: kɑk

## Definition
Gé 閣 refers to the wooden table higher than àn 案 which was put among the seats during the feasts and was used to put on dishes to be eaten later. It can have several levels. Later, this table was also used to put on books.
