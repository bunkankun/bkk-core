## Form
- Orth: [惟](../../graphs/f/f940ab13-99ad-4f21-a27e-217dbe012fc5.md)
  - Pinyin: wéi
  - Old Chinese: k-lul
  - Middle Chinese: ji

## Definition
Classical Chinese had several common copulas at an early stage: wéi 維/唯/惟 (ant. fēi 非 "is not") was common in SHI and SHU, but discontinued later.
