## Form
- Orth: [犯](../../graphs/1/10a01e52-79e7-4ea4-a62c-a4582670745e.md)
  - Pinyin: fàn
  - Old Chinese: bomʔ
  - Middle Chinese: bi̯ɐm

## Definition
Fàn 犯 (ant. cǒng 從 "follow obediently") refers as a derogatory term to a deliberate and active breaking of a rule.
