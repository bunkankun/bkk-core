  - Pinyin: yǐ
  - Old Chinese: qralʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʔiɛ

## Definition
Yǐ 倚 is occasionally used interchangeably with yī 依 (with which it is interdefined in SHUOWEN, but the word typically refers to leaning against something rather than vertically on it.
