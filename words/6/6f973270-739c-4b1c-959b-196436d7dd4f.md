  - Pinyin: zú
  - Old Chinese: skud
  - Middle Chinese: tsʷit

## Definition
Zú 卒 (ant. chū 初 "beginning") typically refers to a definitive and often abrupt end to a process which has lasted some time, but not for all the preceding time.
