- Orth: [索](../../graphs/d/d262d717-20f1-49b4-9803-875a0c8bd00b.md)
  - Pinyin: suǒ
  - Old Chinese: saaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: sɑk

## Definition
Suǒ 索 refers to the rope larger and more crude than shéng 繩; it was usually made not of silk or hemp, but of grass, wooden, or bamboo strips.
