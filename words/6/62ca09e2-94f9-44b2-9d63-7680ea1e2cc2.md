# 阼: STAIRS

- Super-entry: [阼](../../super-entries/c/c6b40b66-466d-4288-b40b-66466d128814.md)
- Concept: [STAIRS](../../concepts/0/07178f82-5672-4502-9797-85c0c58d141b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [阼](../../graphs/5/5d3f8a20-8900-47d7-8faf-55b768b2c996.md)
  - Pinyin: zuò
  - Old Chinese: dzaaɡs
  - Middle Chinese: dzuo̝

## Definition
Zuò 阼 refers to the east-side staircase in a palace, temple, or aristocratic mansion. This was used by the host when inviting guests (while the guests used western staircase) or by the ruler when performing sacrifice.
