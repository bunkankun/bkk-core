# 楹: PILLAR

- Super-entry: [楹](../../super-entries/c/c7d29b70-2e8e-4bb0-929b-702e8eebb014.md)
- Concept: [PILLAR](../../concepts/7/7a83ff00-885d-47b0-98da-0c23c163b5a8.md)

## Form
- Orth: [楹](../../graphs/8/80c99fa1-8f86-4470-8239-7d31ba9da51d.md)
  - Pinyin: yíng
  - Old Chinese: leŋ
  - Middle Chinese: jiɛŋ

## Definition
Yíng 楹 refers specifically to a pillar in front of the main hall, but it can be also used like a general term for a pillar.
