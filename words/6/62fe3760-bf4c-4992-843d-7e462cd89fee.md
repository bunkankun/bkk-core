- Orth: [臣](../../graphs/f/f97584af-067f-4b72-a600-a47df1634908.md)
  - Pinyin: chén
  - Old Chinese: ɡjiŋ

## Definition
Chén 臣 emphasises the subservience of the servant and his lasting employment in a subservient position.
