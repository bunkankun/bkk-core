- Orth: [因](../../graphs/f/fb148467-ef53-4489-8a08-074bfe0f9d69.md)
  - Pinyin: yīn
  - Old Chinese: qin
  - Middle Chinese: ʔin

## Definition
Yīn 因 "on the basis of" always refers to some kind of deliberate accommodation to objective conditions.
