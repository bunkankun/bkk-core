## Form
- Orth: [壯](../../graphs/e/eb613b7b-d261-433b-9919-d4ea065777f8.md)
  - Pinyin: zhuàng
  - Old Chinese: skraŋs
  - Middle Chinese: ʈʂi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Zhuàng 壯 (ant. ruò 弱 "weak") refers to longish-term constitutional physical strength.
