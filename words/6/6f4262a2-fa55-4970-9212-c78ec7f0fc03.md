# 切: RUB

- Super-entry: [切](../../super-entries/c/c02c4339-0a57-4b0a-ac43-390a576b0ae8.md)
- Concept: [RUB](../../concepts/6/6f7adb7e-4a80-498a-9ad8-5886013168de.md)

## Form
- Orth: [切](../../graphs/8/81c28de1-ab95-4916-8f6c-2118471b340d.md)
  - Pinyin: qiē, qiè
  - Old Chinese: snʰiid
  - Middle Chinese: tshet

## Definition
Qǐē 切 adds to the notion of rubbing that of cutting and carving in order to produce smooth surfaces.
