  - Pinyin: kūn dì
  - Old Chinese: kuun liilʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kuo̝n dei

## Definition
Kūn dì 昆弟 refers to brothers in general, but there is a pervasive doubt whether it does not also include the notion of cousins. See COUSIN.
