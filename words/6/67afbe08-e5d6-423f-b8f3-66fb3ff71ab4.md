  - Pinyin: zǎo
  - Old Chinese: tsuuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tsɑu

## Definition
The current word for early, referring both to periods in one's life and time in the year or the day, is zǎo 早 (ant. wǎn 晚 "late"), but the word often specifically means "too early".
