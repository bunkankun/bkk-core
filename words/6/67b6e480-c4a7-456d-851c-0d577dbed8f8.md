  - Pinyin: shàng
  - Old Chinese: djaŋs
  - Middle Chinese: dʑi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Shàng 尚(ant. qīng 輕 "have no special respect for") refers to giving high practical priority to something or someone out of a sense of admiration or appreciation.
