## Definition
Tǎo 討 refers to a an extended punitive campaign by someone who construes himself as being in moral authority and entitled to uphold rectitude and morality through warfare. (Also figurative as in 天討有罪"Heaven punishes those who are guilty". See PUNISH) [DESCENDING+], [MILITARY], [OVERT]
