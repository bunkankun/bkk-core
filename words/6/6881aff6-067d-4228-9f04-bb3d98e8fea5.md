## Form
- Orth: [許](../../graphs/c/cea102cd-f4c1-4145-8afa-fcbd88ec12f1.md)
  - Pinyin: xǔ
  - Old Chinese: hŋaʔ
  - Middle Chinese: hi̯ɤ

## Definition
The current general word for formal permission for someone to do something which he asks to do is xǔ 許 (ant. jìn 禁 "forbid").
