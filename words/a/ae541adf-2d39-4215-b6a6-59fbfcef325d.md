## Form
- Orth: [甘](../../graphs/5/56622cc8-40f8-4c97-906b-df7a4d477b60.md)
  - Pinyin: gān
  - Old Chinese: kaam
  - Middle Chinese: kɑm

## Definition
The current standard word for sweetness is gān 甘, and the word strongly connotes pleasantness of taste rather than just sugary flavour. CQFL: 五味之美也
