## Definition
Zhōng yōng 中庸 "the mean in action" may be mentioned as a a central Confucian virtue, jiān ài 兼愛 "unIversal love" as a Mohist virtue, wú wéi 無為"unobtrusive action" as a non-moralistic Taoist virtue.
