## Definition
NB: Xiè 灺 refers specifically to what is left of candles etc after they are burnt out and came into use in Tang times (Hán Yù).

## Senses
1. **[nm](../../syntactic-functions/e/e917a78b-5500-4276-a5fe-156b8bdecb7b.md)** whatever is left over after burning anything (including a candle etc)
