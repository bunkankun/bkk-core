  - Pinyin: lǜ
  - Old Chinese: b-rud
  - Middle Chinese: lʷin

## Definition
Lǜ 律 is far more specialised as a specific term referring to concrete and detailed regulations rather than a legal system as a whole, and the word is also syntactically much less flexible. Thus we have 先王之法 and not 先王之律.
