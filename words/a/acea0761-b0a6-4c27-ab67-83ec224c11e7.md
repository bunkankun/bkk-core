# 短褐: JACKET

- Super-entry: [短褐](../../super-entries/1/15de626e-9828-4736-9e62-6e982877368c.md)
- Concept: [JACKET](../../concepts/c/c6de422f-347d-4380-abeb-62a79d73cef8.md)

## Form
- Orth: [短](../../graphs/a/a864b05f-aad3-4683-acd2-402a2550a8a5.md)[褐](../../graphs/4/4addf984-2ab9-49ab-8750-1486377feb69.md)
  - Pinyin: duǎn hè
  - Old Chinese: toonʔ ɡaad
  - Middle Chinese: tʷɑn ɦɑt

## Definition
Shù hè 裋褐 "plain jackets" (often written duǎn hè 短褐) commonly refers to rough or plain jackets worn by persons of humble status.

## Senses
1. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** humble clothes (this form which is current in HANSHU may well be the correct way of writing duǎn hè 短裋)
