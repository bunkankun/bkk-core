# 耕: AGRICULTURE

- Super-entry: [耕](../../super-entries/6/6f132116-7a7e-4fab-9321-167a7eefabf1.md)
- Concept: [AGRICULTURE](../../concepts/5/59ca81ce-e357-48fd-a522-d627c0d9f6a3.md)

## Form
- Orth: [耕](../../graphs/8/8418abe9-78bf-4564-8c4c-48e7e5db208a.md)
  - Pinyin: gēng
  - Old Chinese: kreeŋ
  - Middle Chinese: kɣɛŋ

## Definition
Gēng 耕 refers to any agricultural work involved in the growing of crops of any kind; however, the term also refers specifically to ploughing and thus turning over and loosening the ground for cultivation.
