# 求: REQUEST

- Super-entry: [求](../../super-entries/c/cf1b79d1-6d37-4df2-9b79-d16d37bdf2c7.md)
- Concept: [REQUEST](../../concepts/8/8776e03b-d58b-43cd-b694-22237290bbeb.md)

## Form
- Orth: [求](../../graphs/f/f68bbc45-0deb-4d2f-bd88-bef660d91d75.md)
  - Pinyin: qiú
  - Old Chinese: ɡu
  - Middle Chinese: gɨu

## Definition
The current general word for a request is qiú 求 (ant. dé 得 "obtain"), which however can also come to mean demand and is therefore not a particularly polite term to use.
