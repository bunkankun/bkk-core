- Orth: [如](../../graphs/b/b70766fd-8fa3-4174-9134-d39d5f504d70.md)
  - Pinyin: rú
  - Old Chinese: nja
  - Middle Chinese: ȵi̯ɤ

## Definition
Rú 如 refers to equalling something else as it were "from below", coming up to a something else in a certain sense.
