## Definition
Lí 黎/黧 (ant. sù 素 "pristine white and un-suntanned") refers to the dark sunburnt complexion of those who work in the open for long periods. [IDIOMATIC], [LITERAL], [PROSAIC], [SPECIFIC]

## Senses
1. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** of the dark complexion of humans who have been exposed over long time to wind and sun (thus giving rise to the expression lí mín 黎民 for the common people)
