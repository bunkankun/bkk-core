  - Pinyin: pǔ qǐng
  - Old Chinese: phaaʔ tsheŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: phuo̝ tshiɛŋ

## Definition
This word appeared during the Tang and the original meaning is: "to gather the assembly of monks/nuns". In the Chán Buddhist context it assumed the extended meaning of "gathering the monks of a monastery in order to engage in manual labour on the monastery property (fields)".
