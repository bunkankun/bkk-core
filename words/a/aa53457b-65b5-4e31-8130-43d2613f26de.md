## Senses
1. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** semicircular indentures towards the sides of the main lower beam supporting the carriage box. Corresponding protrusions on the zhóu 軸 lock into these protrusions to keep the box stably in the middle and to allow the carriage box to be detached from the main body of the cart.
