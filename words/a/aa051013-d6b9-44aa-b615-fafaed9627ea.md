## Form
- Orth: [殤](../../graphs/d/db8b1129-2a87-4536-95c9-630ca483a9b5.md)
  - Pinyin: shàng
  - Middle Chinese: ɕi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Yāo (old: yǎo) 夭 , yǎo 殀 and shāng 殤 (ant. shòu 壽 "long-lived") refer to an early and not just untimely death.
