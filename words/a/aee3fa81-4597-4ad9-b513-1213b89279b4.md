## Form

  - Pinyin: dàng
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-laaŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dɑŋ

## Definition
Sì 肆 and dàng 蕩 (ant. jū 拘 "be properly restrained") refer often positively to lack of inhibitions and a freedom of behaviour.
