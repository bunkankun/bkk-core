  - Old Chinese: ɢraam
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣam

## Definition
Xián 銜 is the general word for any contraption put into a horse's mouth in order to steer or constrain it. Attached to the xián 銜 is the biāo 鑣"bit-holder".
