  - Old Chinese: ŋɡraam

## Definition
Yán 巖 (or yán 岩) focusses on height and steepness in a rocky landscape.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** probably as a deverbal noun: craggy high mountain; by implication or extension, occasionally: cave in the craggy mountains **[2 Attributions](#c61d86ed-3a90-459e-904a-ffda7470e50b)**
   - Usage: warring-states-currency: 4
2. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** mountainous; often intensitive and reduplicated: craggy, high; (recluses) hidden in the mountains **[4 Attributions](#96d84773-86d9-4af6-89e4-aa9d2ffd48d8)**
   - Usage: warring-states-currency: 4
3. **[vi.red](../../syntactic-functions/e/e627d1e1-0e26-4069-9615-1025ebb7c0a2.md)** *[intensitive](../../semantic-features/a/a24260a1-0410-4d64-acde-5967b1bef725.md)* be impressively craggy **[1 Attributions](#f9b6ccff-ff4f-4d8c-b1a4-3ce4c76e63c2)**
