## Definition
Dà dài 大帶 is a late Warring States term for the shēn 紳 "official belt". [IDIOM], [OFFICIAL], [PROSAIC]

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** silken outer belt only worn by a person of considerable status, used as a sign of status (until the use of this became more common in Sui and Tang times)
