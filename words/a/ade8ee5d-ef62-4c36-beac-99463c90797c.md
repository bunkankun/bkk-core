# 空: EMPTY

- Super-entry: [空](../../super-entries/4/459659e4-5fe7-45f2-9659-e45fe7d5f224.md)
- Concept: [EMPTY](../../concepts/e/e711369f-d210-47c5-9fea-a8ca115a014e.md)

## Form
- Orth: [空](../../graphs/d/d05fe3a9-6525-4d1b-bc3e-677fd903e2dc.md)
  - Pinyin: kōng
  - Old Chinese: khooŋ
  - Middle Chinese: khuŋ

## Definition
Kōng 空 (ant. mǎn 滿 "filled up") generally refers to literal emptiness of a container, but used adjectivally the word also has abstract uses as in kōng yán 空言 "empty words, abstract insubstantial discourse".
