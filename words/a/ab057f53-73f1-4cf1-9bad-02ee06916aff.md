- Orth: [疏](../../graphs/a/a09005af-0806-4a40-bb68-a4edff679243.md)

  - Old Chinese: sqra

## Definition
Shū 疏 (ant. qīn 親 "close") often refers to distance of relation in a more than purely physical sense.
