## Form
- Orth: [闔](../../graphs/0/0f2b7d6a-e13a-4916-a0b0-960ef4f7fd28.md)
  - Pinyin: hé
  - Old Chinese: ɡaab
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɑp

## Definition
Hé 闔 refers generally to the wing of a door. Specifically - according to the Zheng's commentary to the LIJI - the word refers to the wooden wing.
