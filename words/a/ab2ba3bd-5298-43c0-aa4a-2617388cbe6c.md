  - Pinyin: gǎo
  - Old Chinese: khoowʔ
  - Middle Chinese: khɑu

## Definition
Gǎo 槁 refers specifically to the withering of plants, or to their withered state. The combination kū gǎo 枯槁 is idiomatic.
