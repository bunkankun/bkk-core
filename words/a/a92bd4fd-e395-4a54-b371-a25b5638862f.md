## Form
- Orth: [真](../../graphs/d/d4d66e15-3f6d-47b1-adf9-2fee6a70c68e.md)
  - Pinyin: zhēn
  - Old Chinese: tjin
  - Middle Chinese: tɕin

## Definition
The general dominant word for something being not artificial or fake is zhēn 真(ant. wěi 偽 "fake"), but I find is important that the word never seems to apply to negative concepts as in English "genuine hatred".
