- Orth: [飄](../../graphs/1/10507d94-7d3c-4b41-ad41-11f7037ace2d.md)
  - Pinyin: piāo

## Definition
Piāo 飄 and the rare biāo 飆 refer to whirlwind, and the word is also written with two characters as 扶搖.
