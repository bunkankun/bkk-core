  - Old Chinese: sqon
  - Middle Chinese: siɛn

## Definition
Xuān 宣 (ant.* mì 秘 "keep secret") is an announcement to a general public; and the word often refers specifically to royal or imperial promulgation. [DESCENDING], [GENERAL]
