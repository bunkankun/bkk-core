- Super-entry: [恕](../../super-entries/2/20403bc0-9c42-4c77-803b-c09c42fc775b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [恕](../../graphs/3/3136001e-c9fa-4296-a51c-6d0876650b88.md)
  - Pinyin: shù
  - Old Chinese: hnjas
  - Middle Chinese: ɕi̯ɤ

## Definition
Shù 恕 (ant.* chóu 仇 "take a strict hostile attitude to") refers to acts of forgiveness as well as an understanding attitude which involves forgiveness as a virtue.
