## Form
- Orth: [愚](../../graphs/1/1dda875c-1c6f-4cd7-932d-e80e454c7823.md)
  - Pinyin: yú
  - Old Chinese: ŋo
  - Middle Chinese: ŋi̯o

## Definition
The dominant word is yú 愚 (ant. zhì 智 "clever; wise"), and the word refers to intellectual obtuseness as well as practical ineptitude.
