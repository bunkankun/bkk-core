# 眄: LOOK SIDEWAYS

## Form
- Orth: [眄](../../graphs/e/ea718bad-b02b-4b4d-939b-a0acd5ace386.md)
  - Pinyin: miǎn
  - Old Chinese: mbiinʔ
  - Middle Chinese: men

## Definition
Nì 睨 and miǎn 眄 refers to looking at someone sidewise as a show of demonstrative disrespect.
