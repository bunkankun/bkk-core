## Form
- Orth: [慕](../../graphs/2/241399d2-1fb4-47e2-a59b-8a8e60615740.md)
  - Pinyin: mù
  - Old Chinese: maaɡs
  - Middle Chinese: muo̝

## Definition
Mù 慕 prototypically refers to loving devotion of a distant kind, but the word is also used in a more generalised way to refer to affection.
