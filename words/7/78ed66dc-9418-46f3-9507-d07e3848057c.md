  - Pinyin: bǐng
  - Old Chinese: praŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: pɣaŋ

## Definition
Bǐng 秉 refers to clasping something firmly, often solemnly with both hands, and sometimes several things so as to hold and keep them together and not to let go.
