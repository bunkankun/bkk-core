  - Pinyin: chuí
  - Old Chinese: dol
  - Middle Chinese: ɖiɛ

## Definition
Chuí 錘 refers to the weight. According to FANG YAN, in Han times the word was used in the area of ancient states of Lu and Song; later it came to be used as general word for the weight.
