  - Pinyin: yuán
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷen
  - Middle Chinese: ɦiɛn

## Definition
Huán 圜 refers to the round coin with a square hole in a centre which was cast by the states of Qin and Zhou. After Qin unification, it became general currency for a whole empire.
