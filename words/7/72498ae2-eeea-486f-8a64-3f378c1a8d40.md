  - Pinyin: yàng
  - Old Chinese: k-laŋs
  - Middle Chinese: ji̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Yàng 恙 is restricted to the idiomatic formula wú yàng 無恙 "be in good health" and sometimes yǒu yàng 有恙 "have medical problems".
