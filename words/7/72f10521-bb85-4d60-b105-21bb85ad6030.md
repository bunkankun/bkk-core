1. **[NPpr](../../syntactic-functions/c/c43c0bab-2810-42a4-a6be-e4641d9b6632.md)** Duàn Guī 段規 (fl. 453) was a ministerial adviser in Han 韓 to Han Kangzi 韓康子 and was instrumental in the murder of the Earl of Zhi 智伯 in 453. In ZGC Zhao 1 Duan Gui advises the ruler of the state of Han against acceding to demands for land by the Earl of Bo. According to GY Jinyu 9 and SY 5.27 Duan Gui is instrumental in the murder of the Earl. **[1 Attributions](#25de5028-ab6b-4753-9e50-28ab6bd753c9)**
