  - Old Chinese: ɡ-raŋ
  - Middle Chinese: li̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Liáng 涼 (ant. wēn 溫 "lukewarm") describes a mild form of coldness, prototypically that of a cool breeze liáng fēng 涼風.
