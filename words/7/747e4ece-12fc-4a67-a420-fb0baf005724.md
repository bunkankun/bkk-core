# 化: CHANGE

- Super-entry: [化](../../super-entries/e/eb96316c-367c-40dd-9631-6c367c50ddbf.md)

## Form
- Orth: [化](../../graphs/7/7c36ccf6-0da3-4fdf-8873-43b8edf824c7.md)
  - Pinyin: huà
  - Old Chinese: hŋʷraals
  - Middle Chinese: hɣɛ

## Definition
Huà 化 (ant. cháng 常 "remain constant") refers to irreversible change into a fundamentally new state, while retaining one's identity.
