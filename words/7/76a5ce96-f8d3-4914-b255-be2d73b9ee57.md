# 瘞: SACRIFICE

## Form
- Orth: [瘞](../../graphs/f/ff7c82ab-4aa1-4064-bd39-85981cf4a3ee.md)
  - Pinyin: yì
  - Old Chinese: qrebs
  - Middle Chinese: ʔiɛi

## Definition
Yì 瘞 refers to placing something (typically jade, or domestic animals) in the ground as part of or as a form of sacrifice to a deceased person.
