  - Old Chinese: sqaŋs
  - Middle Chinese: si̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Xiàng 相 refers to the head of the group of ministers, the prime minister, also called yǐn 尹 or lìng yǐn 令尹 in the southern state of Chǔ.
