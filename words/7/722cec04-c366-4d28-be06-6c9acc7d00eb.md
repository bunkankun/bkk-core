- Orth: [眾](../../graphs/1/18f9f0fa-f6c8-4b5f-b01e-2eb769c2d2c1.md)
  - Pinyin: zhòng
  - Old Chinese: tjuŋs
  - Middle Chinese: tɕuŋ

## Definition
Zhòng 眾 (ant. guǎ 寡 "few") and zhū 諸 refer to a large number of items of a certain kind.
