## Form
- Orth: [敗](../../graphs/f/f9aa2f5b-6913-43a3-8475-cc24c3832272.md)
  - Pinyin: bài
  - Old Chinese: braads
  - Middle Chinese: bɣɛi

## Definition
Bài 敗 (ant. chéng 成 "achieve one's purpose") construes failure as a defeat in some personal or social undertaking or campaign.
