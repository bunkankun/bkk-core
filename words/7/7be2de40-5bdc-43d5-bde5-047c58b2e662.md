## Definition

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** occasionally, like qiū 丘, used for the tumulus of an imperial tomb, as in 陵山. In 水經注 the word has come to refer specifically to a Han tomb.
