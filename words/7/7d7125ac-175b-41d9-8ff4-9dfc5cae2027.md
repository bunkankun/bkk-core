  - Old Chinese: k-liɡs

## Definition
Zhì 致 sometimes emphasises the difficulty of the task of reaching, but this word too is often hard to distinguish in meaning from 至 as defined above when it means "arrive". [DIFFICULT!], [PROCESS!]
