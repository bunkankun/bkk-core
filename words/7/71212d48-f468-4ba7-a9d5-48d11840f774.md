## Form
- Orth: [恬](../../graphs/8/8ba3ddaf-d60b-47d2-adc2-d40adc66db24.md)
  - Pinyin: tián
  - Old Chinese: b-leem
  - Middle Chinese: dem

## Definition
Tián 恬 and dàn 淡 (all ant. dòng dàng 動蕩 "be all over the place) refer to serenity in an individual as cultivated, notoriously, by the Taoists.
