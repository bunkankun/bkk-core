## Form
- Orth: [寒](../../graphs/2/23b47fd8-2929-424f-b8bc-482da10682d6.md)
  - Pinyin: hán
  - Old Chinese: ɡaan
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɑn

## Definition
The standard current word referring to coldness is hán 寒 (ant. standardly shǔ 暑, but also wēn 溫, rè 熱 "warm").
