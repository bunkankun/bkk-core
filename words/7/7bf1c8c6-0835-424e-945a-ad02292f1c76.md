  - Pinyin: lǐ
  - Old Chinese: riiʔ
  - Middle Chinese: lei

## Definition
Lǐ 禮 (ant. màn 慢 "treat without proper ritual respect") refers to a proper show of public demonstrative respect for someone on the part of a host or a superior.
