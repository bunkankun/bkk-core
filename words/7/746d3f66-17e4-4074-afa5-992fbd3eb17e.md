# 傘: RAIN HAT

## Form
- Orth: [傘](../../graphs/d/d9bb4093-ed79-4a07-b53e-1aa32e0f280a.md)
  - Pinyin: sǎn
  - Old Chinese: saanʔ
  - Middle Chinese: sɑn

## Definition
Sǎn 傘 refers to a square dēng 簦, and from Three Kingdom times onwards these could be opened and closed and developed into the predecessor of the modern umbrella.
