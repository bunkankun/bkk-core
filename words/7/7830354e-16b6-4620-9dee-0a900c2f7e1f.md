## Form
- Orth: [易](../../graphs/7/7c642fc0-0e42-4485-9f2d-5ec254f96e4c.md)
  - Pinyin: yì
  - Old Chinese: leɡ
  - Middle Chinese: jiɛk

## Definition
Yì 易 refers prototypically to a change construed as brought about by a process of interchange and (often mutual) replacement.
