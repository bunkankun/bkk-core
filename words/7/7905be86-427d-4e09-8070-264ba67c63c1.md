  - Pinyin: yòu
  - Old Chinese: qriws
  - Middle Chinese: ʔi̯u

## Definition
The current general "absolute" word for tender youth is yòu 幼 (ant. zhǎng 長 "grown up"), and this word refers to young age of animals, as well as humans.
