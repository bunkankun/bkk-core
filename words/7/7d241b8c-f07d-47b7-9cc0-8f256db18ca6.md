  - Pinyin: láo
  - Old Chinese: ruu
  - Middle Chinese: lɑu

## Definition
Láo 牢 refers to an bovine specially reared for sacrificial person, and the word is ubiquitous in OBI.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** OBI 5: bovine specially reared for sacrificial purposes, and the word also refers to sheep so used, although the graph is sometimes varied by putting the sheep radical instead of the ox under the roof. Xu's dictionary writes, disarmingly: 用於祭祀之牛羊
