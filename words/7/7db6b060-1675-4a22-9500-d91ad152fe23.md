## Form
- Orth: [明](../../graphs/5/5ed07350-e3b9-46dc-a120-719ce838ad97.md)
  - Pinyin: míng
  - Old Chinese: mraŋ
  - Middle Chinese: mɣaŋ

## Definition
Míng 明 is to throw intellectual light on something by any means, including illustration as well as historical explanation or definition.
