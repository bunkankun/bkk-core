  - Pinyin: zhēn
  - Old Chinese: krlim
  - Middle Chinese: ʈim

## Definition
砧 zhēn is a post Qín 秦 word for a block used to beat a silk and other fabrics; it can also refer to a block for executions by beheading or cutting in the waist.
