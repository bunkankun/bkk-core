# 忠: FAITHFUL

- Super-entry: [忠](../../super-entries/f/f826df54-866c-4c84-a6df-54866cfc84ab.md)
- Concept: [FAITHFUL](../../concepts/2/2bb571aa-4731-4d39-adb2-4a5e604300a5.md)

## Form
- Orth: [忠](../../graphs/8/80293169-a9df-4ca9-b526-432fdd2fa02e.md)
  - Pinyin: zhōng
  - Old Chinese: krluŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈuŋ

## Definition
Zhōng 忠 refers to a selfless effort on behalf of the person to whom one takes oneself to owe loyalty, and this person may be either a ruler or a friend.
