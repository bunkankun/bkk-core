# 救: FORGIVE

- Super-entry: [救](../../super-entries/0/0d0f8347-3dad-4a0b-8f83-473dad6a0b25.md)
- Concept: [FORGIVE](../../concepts/a/a7781b07-2b28-4722-910c-4465de00a1ef.md)

## Form
- Orth: [救](../../graphs/8/8a5f773f-db22-4cca-b25f-59bd280a57e6.md)
  - Pinyin: jiù
  - Old Chinese: kus
  - Middle Chinese: kɨu

## Definition
Jiù 救 literally refers to the act of forgiving insofar as it constitutes an act of saving the forgivee from the results of his transgression which might ensue if he were not to be forgiven.

## Senses
1. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** forgive so as to rescue (see also 救赦）
