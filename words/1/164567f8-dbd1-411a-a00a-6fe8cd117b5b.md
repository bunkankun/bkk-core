  - Old Chinese: ɢljɯɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ʑɨk

## Definition
The common general term for an eclipse is shí 蝕 (the earlier form being shí 食). It can refer both to a solar eclipse (rì shí 日蝕) or lunar eclipse (yuè shí 月蝕).
