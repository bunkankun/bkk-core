## Form
- Orth: [免](../../graphs/3/34a4064e-5272-4aea-a8e8-4a5e5bdef114.md)
  - Pinyin: miǎn
  - Old Chinese: mronʔ
  - Middle Chinese: miɛn

## Definition
The general word is miǎn 免 (ant. zāo 遭 "encounter"), and this refers to any voluntary or involuntary process leading to one's escape from what otherwise might occur, especially one's being spared a negative experience. [CONCRETE], [GENERAL]
