# 銜: HOLD IN MOUTH

- Super-entry: [銜](../../super-entries/6/6dc95717-b098-440c-8957-17b098440c5f.md)
- Concept: [HOLD IN MOUTH](../../concepts/5/5647994e-a268-47ad-ada3-2c70d3fcbf9d.md)

## Form
- Orth: [銜](../../graphs/d/d0065c51-75b9-4ded-b8f3-dbee6f4c7a7a.md)
  - Pinyin: xián
  - Old Chinese: ɢraam
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣam

## Definition
Xián 銜 refers to holding something in one's mouth with part of the object remaining visible from the outside.

## Senses
1. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** hold an object in the mouth so that part of it remains visible, hence: harbour inside one (mostly negative sentiments), harbour resentment **[6 Attributions](#e3ed3ff3-759a-48a0-91a3-41ba30e3b06d)**
2. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** *[figurative](../../semantic-features/2/2e48851c-928e-40f0-ae0d-2bf3eafeaa17.md)* hold in the mouth
3. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** *[continuous](../../semantic-features/1/1e331347-13e3-42a1-a1a8-8e4404f03509.md)* against one's will: have in one's mouth **[1 Attributions](#949ef852-75f7-45c5-9ef8-5275f7b5c550)**
