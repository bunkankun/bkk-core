## Form
- Orth: [淪](../../graphs/f/f5dced5b-31ea-414f-800b-1b5afeab85f7.md)
  - Pinyin: lún
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-run
  - Middle Chinese: lʷin

## Definition
Lún 淪 "sink into" (ant. fàn 汎 "float") has abstract usages where the word means "go under" and gets close in meaning to wáng 亡 "be ruined".
