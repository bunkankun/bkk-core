  - Old Chinese: lɯŋs
  - Middle Chinese: jɨŋ

## Definition
Dì 娣 and yìng 媵 refer to younger sisters who follow along with their elder sisters marrying their elder sister's husband as secondary wives.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** subsidiary wives or concubines that follow along with the main woman one is marrying
