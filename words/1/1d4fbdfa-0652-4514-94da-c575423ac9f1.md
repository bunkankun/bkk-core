  - Old Chinese: dɯŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɖɨŋ

## Definition
Qīng 清 (ant. zhuó 濁 "dirty, impure") and the rarer chéng 澂 emphasises the absence of any admixtures that might make something less transparent and translucent.
