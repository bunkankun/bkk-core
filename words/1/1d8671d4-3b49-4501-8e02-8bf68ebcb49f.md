  - Old Chinese: tsuu

## Definition
Zāo 遭 refers to being exposed to something or running into someone, and the focus is on the fact that the encounter is generally hostile (遭宋司馬，將要而殺之 "ran into the Marshal of Sòng and wanted to kill him").
