# 王: KING

- Super-entry: [王](../../super-entries/f/f0240ba0-efbf-40cf-a40b-a0efbf40cf3c.md)

## Form
- Orth: [王](../../graphs/3/3b611bc0-1264-4fb0-b354-69ff386f2094.md)
  - Pinyin: wáng
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɦi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
The general term for a king of any kind is wáng 王, and in Warring States times this term can refer to all sovereign rulers, even the Son of Heaven.
