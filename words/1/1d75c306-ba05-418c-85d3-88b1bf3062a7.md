## Form
- Orth: [宦](../../graphs/c/c8c7711f-c417-4098-82e1-70e33aa4627b.md)
  - Pinyin: huàn
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷraans
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣan

## Definition
Huàn 宦 refers at an early stage to the person in charge of the servant corps in a household; in imperial times the word came to refer to the head of the imperial household. In later Han times the word came to refer to eunuchs.
