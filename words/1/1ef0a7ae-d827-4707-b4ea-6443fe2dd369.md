## Form
- Orth: [驚](../../graphs/5/5021a57e-244e-42b1-9b17-be32e70ee50f.md)
  - Pinyin: jīng
  - Old Chinese: kreŋ
  - Middle Chinese: kɣaŋ

## Definition
Jīng 驚 refers to the sudden onset of fear when someone is faced with an unexpected danger.
