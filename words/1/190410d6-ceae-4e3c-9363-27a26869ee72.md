## Definition
Méng 氓/萌 (ant.* shì mín 士民 "citizens") refers specifically to the common people belonging to the lower echelons of society.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** *[plural](../../semantic-features/5/5fae11b4-4f4e-441e-8dc7-4ddd74b68c2e.md)* immigrant people, non-native Han people; common people. Can be used synonymously with 民, to denote the mass of ignorant agricultural labourers, and, in a wider use, the governed generally. There is no evidence of use to denote masses of people uprooted from their home because of natural disaster or war, 氓 were in other words not "流氓."
