## Form
- Orth: [忽](../../graphs/b/b17a37e7-db42-4971-8020-af720f20df6f.md)
  - Pinyin: hū
  - Old Chinese: hmɯɯd
  - Middle Chinese: huot

## Definition
The general word for neglecting something, either by deliberately ignoring it or by a failure to take due care, is hū 忽 (ant. jǐn 謹 "pay careful attention to").
