## Form
- Orth: [反](../../graphs/0/0f61b452-d458-4047-a533-8bf1a63b9cb3.md)
  - Pinyin: fǎn
  - Old Chinese: panʔ
  - Middle Chinese: pi̯ɐn

## Definition
Fǎn 反 (ant. zhōng 忠 "show proper loyalty") refers to a political revolt on a lesser scale than luàn 亂, and this word always has negative overtones.
