# 門: GATE

- Super-entry: [門](../../super-entries/f/fa9e1bef-bab8-45cb-9e1b-efbab825cb33.md)
- Concept: [GATE](../../concepts/1/1874c354-c565-45dd-9a66-e8d63df066c8.md)

## Form
- Orth: [門](../../graphs/8/881e0bff-679d-4b37-b2df-2c1f6074f44b.md)
  - Pinyin: mén
  - Old Chinese: mɯɯn
  - Middle Chinese: muo̝n

## Definition
The current general word for a gate or door of any kind is mén 門, but the word also refers specifically to the two-leaf main gate rather than small doors inside a building complex.
