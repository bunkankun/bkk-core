  - Pinyin: fèi
  - Old Chinese: pods
  - Middle Chinese: pi̯ɐi

## Definition
Fèi 廢 (ant. cún 存 "keep and avoid spending") refers to an inclination to use large sums without necessarily indicating that such large expenditure is wasteful.
