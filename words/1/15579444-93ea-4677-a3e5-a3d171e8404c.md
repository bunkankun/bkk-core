  - Old Chinese: ɢaaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɑŋ

## Definition
The current general word for a row is háng 行 (ant.** sǎn 散 "not forming any rows"), which typically refers to a row of things arrayed vertically, but the word also specifically to battle arrays.
