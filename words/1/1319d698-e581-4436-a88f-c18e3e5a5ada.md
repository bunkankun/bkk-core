## Form
- Orth: [少](../../graphs/6/6cafdf64-808b-426b-b319-4a26a7790be7.md)
  - Pinyin: shǎo
  - Old Chinese: hmljewʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ɕiɛu

## Definition
The current general word referring to the relatively small number or the small amount of something is shǎo 少 (ant. duō 多 "many, much").
