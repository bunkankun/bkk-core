## Form
- Orth: [難](../../graphs/e/e789dee5-fd16-4331-bd2b-749ed2f16a58.md)
  - Pinyin: nán
  - Old Chinese: mɢlaan
  - Middle Chinese: nɑn

## Definition
The clearly dominant word for anything that is hard to achieve or hard to sustain is nán 難 (ant. yì 易 "easy").
