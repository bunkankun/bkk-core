# 再拜: BOW DOWN

## Form

## Senses
1. **[VPadV](../../syntactic-functions/8/819e81af-c978-4931-8fd2-52680e097f01.md)** with a double deep bow to show one's polite gratitude (note that 再拜受之 could be taken as two verbs in series, but a lingering doubt remains, since 再拜 so often precedes main verbs, and is never modified by an adverb. 待考) **[6 Attributions](#1c6da66e-0377-449e-9477-cdbe1f24c0d4)**
2. **[VPi](../../syntactic-functions/0/091af450-64e0-4b82-98a2-84d0444b6d19.md)** *[act](../../semantic-features/f/f55cff2f-f0e3-4f08-a89c-5d08fcf3fe89.md)* make a double bow as a show of polite respect (often followed by a knocking of one's head on the ground) **[17 Attributions](#3277f212-2037-4484-920f-40c8a5539fbe)**
3. **[VPtoN](../../syntactic-functions/9/98f2ce75-ae37-4667-90ff-f418c4aeaa33.md)** make a polite double bow addressed to (note that this could be parsed as "bowed to him twice", and this is quite probably the correct analysis.) **[2 Attributions](#c285084b-1ce6-4660-93c6-dc88ff0ba531)**
4. **[VPt(oN)](../../syntactic-functions/5/5b3376f4-75c4-4047-94eb-fc6d1bca520d.md)** bow twice in the direction of the contextually determinate N **[1 Attributions](#1b92b2bc-324b-4f32-92b2-bc324bdf3258)**
