## Form
- Orth: [書](../../graphs/7/7cc155d0-dae4-4325-8ad0-e09ed5a1822e.md)
  - Pinyin: shū
  - Old Chinese: qhlja
  - Middle Chinese: ɕi̯ɤ

## Definition
The standard current word for writing something down or noting it down is shū 書, and this word can never refer to an act of literary creation.
