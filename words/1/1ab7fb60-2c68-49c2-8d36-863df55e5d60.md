  - Pinyin: xiǎng
  - Old Chinese: qhaŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: hi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Xiǎng 響, which primarily means "echo", often comes to refer to any sound without any connotations concerning the irritating nature of the sound.
