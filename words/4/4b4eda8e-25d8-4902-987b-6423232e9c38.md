# 實: REALITY

- Super-entry: [實](../../super-entries/f/f604a8fd-5ab7-44db-84a8-fd5ab734db40.md)
- Concept: [REALITY](../../concepts/f/fd2ccb7b-536c-4569-87bd-40536d6fdf9e.md)

## Form
- Orth: [實](../../graphs/5/5cf5c7be-7e82-4f71-b699-8bfb95517223.md)
  - Pinyin: shí
  - Old Chinese: ɢljiɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ʑit

## Definition
The current word referring to reality is shí 實 (ant. xū 虛 "pretended reality"), but the word acquired this meaning rather late (meaning "object" in earlier texts).
