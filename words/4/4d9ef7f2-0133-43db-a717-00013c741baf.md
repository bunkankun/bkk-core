  - Pinyin: xīn
  - Old Chinese: slɯm
  - Middle Chinese: sim

## Definition
The general word for the attitude of an individual or of a group of people is xīn 心, and the attitude denoted by this word may change in the course of time. [GENERAL], [VAGUE]
