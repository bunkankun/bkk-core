## Form
- Orth: [皮](../../graphs/a/a2f8f8a7-20bd-4c22-b35c-3af8f5514149.md)
  - Pinyin: pí
  - Old Chinese: bral
  - Middle Chinese: biɛ

## Definition
The general word is pí 皮 which refers to all aspects of the skin, typically skin of animals with its natural fur.
