# 必: SHOULD

- Super-entry: [必](../../super-entries/3/3094f4d4-6b6e-4cbf-94f4-d46b6e1cbfcf.md)
- Concept: [SHOULD](../../concepts/d/d0fba9df-4d64-437e-a88e-9f848207a2ce.md)

## Form
- Orth: [必](../../graphs/2/25996ba8-1e36-4438-8c90-d9a399341f8e.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: piɡ
  - Middle Chinese: pit

## Definition
Bì 必 emphasises obligation as ineluctable and expresses a stronger obligation than bù kě bù 不可不.
