## Form
- Orth: [端](../../graphs/b/b0f78e9d-8436-4cbe-a110-9a39cac62d04.md)
  - Pinyin: duān
  - Old Chinese: toon
  - Middle Chinese: tʷɑn

## Definition
Duān 端 (ant. xié 斜 "slanted") and zhēn 貞 (ant. xié 邪 "morally warped") refer to straightness and non-crookedness, often as a property of abstract things.
