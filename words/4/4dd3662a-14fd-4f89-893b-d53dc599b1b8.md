## Definition
Yán 妍 "attractive and exquisite (of humans as well as human products)" (ant. chì 蚩 "unattractive") refers to elaborate beauty, often close to the modern "sexiness". See SEXY. [ARCHAIC], [ELEVATED], [HUMAN], [POETIC]; [[RARE]]
