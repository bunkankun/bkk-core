- Orth: [帶](../../graphs/3/36dc239a-e19e-4903-b5e5-9270a9bfe777.md)
  - Pinyin: dài
  - Old Chinese: taads
  - Middle Chinese: tɑi

## Definition
Dài 帶 is specifically to carry things along on one's belt, but the word is used more generally for "take along" also in ancient texts.
