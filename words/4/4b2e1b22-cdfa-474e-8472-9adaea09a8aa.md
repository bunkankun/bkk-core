  - Pinyin: shì
  - Old Chinese: qhljiɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ɕit

## Definition
The general word for a room within a house is presumably shì 室, but note in traditional Chinese architecture many kinds of buildings may consist of exactly one room so that the distinction between house and room becomes blurred.
