## Form
- Orth: [恨](../../graphs/3/38a37686-ff74-48ab-ad6e-c613fd21bf24.md)
  - Pinyin: hèn
  - Old Chinese: ɡɯɯns
  - Middle Chinese: ɦən

## Definition
The current general word for regret of one's actions is huǐ 悔, whereas the current general word for regretting past experience is hèn 恨.
