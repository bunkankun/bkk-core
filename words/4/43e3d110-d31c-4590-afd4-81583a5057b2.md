## Form
- Orth: [絕](../../graphs/5/5590ad14-e0fb-4edc-996b-f5b7b83e7d5c.md)
  - Pinyin: jué
  - Old Chinese: dzod
  - Middle Chinese: dziɛt

## Definition
Jué 絕 refers - often metaphorically - to causing something to become discontinuous, to be interrupted or broken off.
