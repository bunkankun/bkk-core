## Form
- Orth: [習](../../graphs/d/d3c78047-6be1-4ede-b366-cc75b701bc2c.md)
  - Pinyin: xí
  - Old Chinese: sɢlɯb
  - Middle Chinese: zip

## Definition
Xí 習 refers to the habitual repeated practice of something in order to achieve proficiency in the kind of action concerned.
