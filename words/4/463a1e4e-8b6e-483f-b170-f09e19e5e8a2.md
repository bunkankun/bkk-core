# 再: REPEAT

- Super-entry: [再](../../super-entries/d/d22a5501-4b4d-4e98-aa55-014b4d1e981f.md)
- Concept: [REPEAT](../../concepts/e/e03e06fd-c039-47fc-a9b4-b439babd78a0.md)

## Form
- Orth: [再](../../graphs/8/89ee3917-1773-426c-a8bd-58c69993a9df.md)
  - Pinyin: zài
  - Old Chinese: tsɯɯs
  - Middle Chinese: tsəi

## Definition
Zài 再 is primarily to do something twice, but the word can also refer to an action being performed for a second time.
