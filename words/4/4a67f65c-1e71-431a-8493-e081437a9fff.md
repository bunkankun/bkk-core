## Form
- Orth: [留](../../graphs/0/002b870a-8d76-48f9-b5af-4d81bf229ddd.md)
  - Pinyin: liú
  - Old Chinese: b-ru
  - Middle Chinese: lɨu

## Definition
Liú 留 (ant. lí 離 "get away from a place") refers to immobility in one place after one has already spent some time there.
