# 恐: FEAR

- Super-entry: [恐](../../super-entries/1/1bea9285-a87f-4acc-aa92-85a87f2acc12.md)
- Concept: [FEAR](../../concepts/8/8ab808df-97f3-40fd-9b35-a9fc0a7aee53.md)

## Form
- Orth: [恐](../../graphs/e/e60a2043-d478-458f-b54c-e0136f8d5d5b.md)
  - Pinyin: kǒng
  - Old Chinese: khoŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: khi̯oŋ

## Definition
Kǒng 恐 refers primarily to intense or overwhelming possibly passing state of fear, and the word differs from jù 懼 mainly in degree of intensity and in the managability of the danger faced.
