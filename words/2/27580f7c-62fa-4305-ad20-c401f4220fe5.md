## Form
- Orth: [垢](../../graphs/8/86d42ba3-024e-413f-b98b-83fbeee99c93.md)
  - Pinyin: gòu
  - Old Chinese: kooʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ku

## Definition
Gòu 垢 refers to the presence of physical dirt on some surface.
