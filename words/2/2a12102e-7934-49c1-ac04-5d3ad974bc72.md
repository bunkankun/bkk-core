## Form
- Orth: [斜](../../graphs/6/6f734512-8943-44e2-b228-3d05fec7dead.md)
  - Pinyin: xié
  - Old Chinese: lja
  - Middle Chinese: zɣɛ

## Definition
Xié 斜 (ant. zhèng 正 "straight and level") refers to any concrete object or movement being neither upright nor horizontal, and the word is first attested in HUANGDINEIJING.

## Senses
1. **[vi](../../syntactic-functions/c/c20780b3-41f9-491b-bb61-a269c1c4b48f.md)** post-Han: slanted "askance"
2. **[vi](../../syntactic-functions/c/c20780b3-41f9-491b-bb61-a269c1c4b48f.md)** *[change](../../semantic-features/3/3d95d354-0c16-419f-9baf-f1f6cb6fbd07.md)* tilt to one side
3. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** SUWEN tilted
4. **[nab](../../syntactic-functions/7/76be1df4-3d73-4e5f-bbc2-729542645bc8.md)** *[mathematical term](../../semantic-features/b/b110bae1-02d5-4c66-ad13-7c04b3ee3ad9.md)* CHEMLA 2003: neither horizontal nor vertical straight line or plane. The word is also used to refer to the hypotenuse. JZ 4.24, Liu Hui's comm: 大弦則中立方之長邪，邪即丸徑也 "The larger hypotenuse being then the longer diagonal of the inscribed cube, the diagonal is hence the diameter of the sphere."
5. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** *[mathematical term](../../semantic-features/b/b110bae1-02d5-4c66-ad13-7c04b3ee3ad9.md)* CHEMLA 2003: (of planes or lines:) in a non-horizontal and non-vertical direction. The word is the antonym both of zhèng 正 "straight/upright" and píng 平 "plane". See also 邪田. JZ 5.15, Liu Hui's comm: 邪解 "disect (a geometrical solid) in a neither horizontal nor vertical direction"
