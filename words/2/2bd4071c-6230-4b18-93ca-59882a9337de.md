## Form

  - Pinyin: yí dí
  - Old Chinese: li deeɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ji dek

## Definition
Yí and Dí barbarians> by metonymy: barbarians in general. Perhaps the most current general word for peoples who were taken not to belong to the Huáxiá 華夏 community is yí dí 夷狄.
