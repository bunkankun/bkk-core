## Definition
Bó 鎛refers to a kind of suspended bell which is similar to zhōng 鐘/鍾 but even larger. Bó 鎛 were suspended not in sets, but alone or in pairs. [UNGER 1995: 376; ill.: MUSIC 2000: 2-4]
