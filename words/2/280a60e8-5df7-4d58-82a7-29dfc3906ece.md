## Form
- Orth: [無](../../graphs/5/5de002ac-c1a1-4519-a177-4a3afcc155bb.md)[為](../../graphs/7/7dd1780c-ee9b-4eaa-af63-c42cb57baf50.md)
  - Pinyin: wú wéi
  - Old Chinese: ma ɢʷal
  - Middle Chinese: mi̯o ɦiɛ

## Definition
Wú wéi 無為 (ant. yǒu suǒ wéi 有所為 "engage in assertive action") is a technical philosophical term referring to the refusal to take assertive action.
