  - Pinyin: dào
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-luuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dɑu

## Definition
Dào 道 "speak of with authority, speak of adequately; quote" often involves quotation or especially serious discussion of important points, or the relating of important evidence.
