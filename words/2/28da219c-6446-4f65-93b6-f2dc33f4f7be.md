  - Pinyin: fá
  - Old Chinese: bod
  - Middle Chinese: bi̯ɐt

## Definition
Fá 伐 refers to a large-scale typically destructive formal attack by one state on another, typically formally announced, and with much beating of drums. [DESCENDING], [MILITARY], [LARGE-SCALE!], [OVERT]
