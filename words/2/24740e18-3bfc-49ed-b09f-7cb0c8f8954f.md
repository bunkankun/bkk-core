## Form
- Orth: [備](../../graphs/d/dc2dae2f-b35f-4be0-bfe8-e8e4cce3bf63.md)
  - Pinyin: bèi
  - Old Chinese: brɯɡs
  - Middle Chinese: bi

## Definition
The current general word for preparing something or supplying something in good time is bèi 備, and what is thus supplied is typically part of some larger scheme.
