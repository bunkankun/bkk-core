- Orth: [造](../../graphs/b/b8f8cfa5-178f-45ab-a2ed-a9ef4e5a0122.md)
  - Pinyin: zào
  - Old Chinese: skhuuɡs
  - Middle Chinese: tshɑu

## Definition
Cào/zào 造 and rú 如 refer to aiming for a destination, and the movement may be by foot or by any conveyance.
