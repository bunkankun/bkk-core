# 窗: WINDOW

- Super-entry: [窗](../../super-entries/a/ac833ae1-3803-4d37-833a-e138032d3749.md)
- Concept: [WINDOW](../../concepts/7/7fd63da8-31ea-4727-89b9-e5cca359f424.md)

## Form
- Orth: [窗](../../graphs/e/e390df87-b121-42bc-acb4-c5c41fedb8a8.md)
  - Pinyin: chuāng
  - Old Chinese: skhrooŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈʂhɣɔŋ

## Definition
Chuāng 窗 refers to a window in the roof, but at later stages comes to replace yǒu 牖 as the standard general word for any window.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** window in the roof of a house (which in ancient times might be dug about one meter into the ground) **[1 Attributions](#fe2e0113-1590-4a63-8cde-14862ba09967)**
   - Usage: warring-states-currency: 4
