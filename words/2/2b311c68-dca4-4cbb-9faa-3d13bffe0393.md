# 卻: WITHDRAW

- Super-entry: [卻](../../super-entries/b/b705be47-2a39-4e66-85be-472a39ae66ef.md)
- Concept: [WITHDRAW](../../concepts/e/e732a131-a134-47a5-9d42-53b7a9cd1660.md)

## Form
- Orth: [卻](../../graphs/c/c13e9847-d859-4e08-8257-41148a9a378c.md)
  - Pinyin: què
  - Old Chinese: khaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: khi̯ɐk

## Definition
Què 卻(ant. qián 前 "move forward") refers to backing off, often under pressure, and typically for a shorter distance, and usually the immediate purpose of the withdrawal is not that of returning to one's point of departure but to get out of a confrontation.
