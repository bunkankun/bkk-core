  - Pinyin: guān
  - Old Chinese: koon
  - Middle Chinese: kʷɑn

## Definition
The general word for inspection are jiān 監 "survey and inspect by higher orders" and guān 觀 which refers very generally to surveying anything, and not necessarily by order of anyone else, but usually in great detail.
