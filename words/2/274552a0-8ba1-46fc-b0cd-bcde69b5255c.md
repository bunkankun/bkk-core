  - Pinyin: xīn
  - Old Chinese: slɯm
  - Middle Chinese: sim

## Definition
The general word for the mental sphere of man is xīn 心 "HEART> mind" (ant. xíng 形 "physical shape, body", tǐ 體 "limbs, body").
