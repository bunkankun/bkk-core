## Definition
Kān 堪 typically refers to the ability to do something others might not be willing or psychologically able to do. ABSOLUTE, CURRENT, DIFFICULT, EMOTIONAL, ENDOGENIC, INHERENT, INNATE, LASTING, PSYCHOLOGICAL.
