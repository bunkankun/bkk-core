## Form
- Orth: [朝](../../graphs/c/c6f40897-559a-4c6a-86d8-d9d87fbf8c55.md)
  - Pinyin: cháo
  - Old Chinese: daw
  - Middle Chinese: ɖiɛu

## Definition
Cháo 朝 refers to the court as the venue of court asemblies rather than as the building as such. See also TEMPLE
