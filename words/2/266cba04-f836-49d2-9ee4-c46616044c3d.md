## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** semicircular indentures in the middle the main lower beam supporting the carriage box. Corresponding protrusions on the zhóu 軸 lock into these protrusions to keep the box stably in the middle and to allow the carriage box to be detached from the main body of the cart.
