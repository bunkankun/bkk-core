# 必: CERTAIN

- Super-entry: [必](../../super-entries/3/3094f4d4-6b6e-4cbf-94f4-d46b6e1cbfcf.md)
- Concept: [CERTAIN](../../concepts/d/d03f8635-80dc-4b4c-a5d0-e367096493e8.md)

## Form
- Orth: [必](../../graphs/2/25996ba8-1e36-4438-8c90-d9a399341f8e.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: piɡ
  - Middle Chinese: pit

## Definition
The current word for certainty of any kind is bì 必, but the word can also refer to logical necessity.
