  - Old Chinese: ɢjuns
  - Middle Chinese: ʑʷin

## Definition
Shùn 順 (ant. nì 逆 "in conflict with the natural flow of events") refers to action that fits naturally and painlessly into a certain flow of events or into a certain constellation. [DYNAMIC]
