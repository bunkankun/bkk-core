  - Pinyin: hóu
  - Old Chinese: ɡoo
  - Middle Chinese: ɦu

## Definition
Hóu 侯 refers specifically to someone who is enfeoffed with a kingdom (or by Han times a minor administrative area) by the Son of Heaven as a hereditary feudal lord.
