## Form
- Orth: [塞](../../graphs/c/c63a43b9-b557-4a93-974b-4d60ba1b171b.md)
  - Pinyin: sài
  - Old Chinese: sɯɯɡs
  - Middle Chinese: səi

## Definition
Sài 塞 emphasises the nuance of filling in a space not necessarily designed to be filled in so as to block the free passage of other substances.
