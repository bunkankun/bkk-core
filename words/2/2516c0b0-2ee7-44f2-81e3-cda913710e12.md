# 暗: DARK

- Super-entry: [暗](../../super-entries/c/c0c81b97-5fd4-4b43-881b-975fd4cb43c2.md)

## Form
- Orth: [暗](../../graphs/7/7be6ddf6-c22a-4420-9dcf-e3741d310346.md)
  - Pinyin: àn
  - Old Chinese: qɯɯms
  - Middle Chinese: ʔəm

## Definition
Àn 暗/闇 (ant. míng 明 "bright") can refer to darkness but most of the time this word actually refers abstractly to the benightedness of a ruler.
