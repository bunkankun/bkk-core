## Form
- Orth: [疑](../../graphs/7/7a60cff2-ff28-4f36-b772-a23cd7e517bd.md)
  - Pinyin: yí
  - Old Chinese: ŋɡɯ
  - Middle Chinese: ŋɨ

## Definition
The current general word for doubting or suspecting is yí 疑 (ant. xìn 信 "have faith in"), and the connotation is always negative. Contrast Latin dubito which is often used in positive senses.
