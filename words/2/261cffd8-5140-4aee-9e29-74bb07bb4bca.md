## Form
- Orth: [展](../../graphs/c/c24021cd-3497-4d2d-84c7-f899ff352eaf.md)
  - Pinyin: zhǎn
  - Old Chinese: tenʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈiɛn

## Definition
Zhǎn 展 (ant. juǎn 卷 "roll up, fold up") specifically refers to the stretching out of things in several directions at the same time.
