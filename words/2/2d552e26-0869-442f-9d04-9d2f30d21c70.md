## Form
- Orth: [順](../../graphs/2/2cb6c010-78ed-44d1-a93d-ced247825273.md)
  - Pinyin: shùn
  - Old Chinese: ɢjuns
  - Middle Chinese: ʑʷin

## Definition
Shùn 順 (ant. nì 逆 "refuse to conform") can refer to the conforming with a standard set by someone or an intention someone has.
