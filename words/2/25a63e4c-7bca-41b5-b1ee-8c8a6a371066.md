## Form
- Orth: [說](../../graphs/0/08ee826a-8ac2-45df-9f16-72515d87430c.md)
  - Pinyin: shuō
  - Old Chinese: lʰod
  - Middle Chinese: ɕiɛt

## Definition
The current general word for a discursive "analytical" explanation of something that is not sufficiently clear in itself is shuō 說.
