- Orth: [危](../../graphs/b/b605a279-35b6-4a82-80c3-3fc922706fef.md)
  - Pinyin: wēi
  - Old Chinese: ŋɡrol
  - Middle Chinese: ŋiɛ

## Definition
The current standard general word for real objective as well as perceived imminent danger is wēi 危 (ant. ān 安 "peace").
