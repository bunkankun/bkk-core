  - Old Chinese: ŋɡaans
  - Middle Chinese: ŋɑn

## Definition
Àn 犴 refers originally to a fierce dog a statue of which was a guardian at prisons, but the name of this dog was then occasionally used by synecdoche as a word for a prison.
