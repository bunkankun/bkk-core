  - Pinyin: yǐ
  - Old Chinese: k-lɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: jɨ

## Definition
Definitive discontinuation, often of inanimate processes, is yǐ 已 (ant. zuò 作 "break into action"). See FINISH.
