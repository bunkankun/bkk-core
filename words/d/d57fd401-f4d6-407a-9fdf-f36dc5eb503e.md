# 背: REVOLT

- Super-entry: [背](../../super-entries/2/249e9f47-c463-4dde-9e9f-47c4634dde9b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [背](../../graphs/c/ccaa847d-d1b7-4e10-ba46-09a96b2e4f60.md)
  - Pinyin: bèi
  - Old Chinese: bɯɯɡs
  - Middle Chinese: buo̝i

## Definition
Bèi 背 and bèi 倍 (ant. shùn 順 "remain loyal") refer to a defiant act of disobedience and turning against one's superiors without a necessary intention to topple these authorities.
