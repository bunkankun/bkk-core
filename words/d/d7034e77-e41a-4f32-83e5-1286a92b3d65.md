  - Pinyin: yǒng
  - Old Chinese: k-loŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ji̯oŋ

## Definition
Yǒng 踊(later also written 踴) refers to the action of jumping up in one place,without moving forward, mostly as a sign of mourning.
