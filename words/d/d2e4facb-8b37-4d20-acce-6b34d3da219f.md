## Form

  - Pinyin: xù
  - Old Chinese: ljaʔ
  - Middle Chinese: zi̯ɤ

## Definition
Xù 敘 refers to laying out something in an orderly manner, often in conversation with equals and in a confidential context, and the word became current in Han times.

## Senses
1. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** GY: give an orderly account of (for information); lay out in proper sequence
