- Super-entry: [前](../../super-entries/5/5eab2483-6c71-492a-ab24-836c71d92ae9.md)

## Form
- Orth: [前](../../graphs/3/3c737232-43d1-4954-a944-3c239391744c.md)
  - Pinyin: qián
  - Old Chinese: dzeen
  - Middle Chinese: dzen

## Definition
The current word for "before in space" is qián 前 (ant. hòu 後"behind"), and the word does not seem to have competitors.
