## Senses
1. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** the multitude of living creatures; BUDDH: the multitude of sentient beings (this is the Buddhist standard term for animate beings; usually referring to human beings but basically also including animals and sometimes even vegetation); skr. sattva **[16 Attributions](#80c36647-c67c-4ba8-a2b8-cb65a4df6b46)**
2. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** *[referential](../../semantic-features/f/ff802381-5859-48eb-909a-e937d69218c6.md)* BUDDH: sentient being (this is an extraordinary case where 眾生 is used referential and in the singular!); skr. sattva **[1 Attributions](#da77d43f-db65-49f4-8922-cac5a69bff43)**
3. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** *[plural](../../semantic-features/5/5fae11b4-4f4e-441e-8dc7-4ddd74b68c2e.md)* BUDDH: the multitude of sentient beings (contextually identifyable and specified) (this is the Buddhist standard term for animate beings; usually referring to human beings but basically also including animals and sometimes even vegetation); skr. sattva **[1 Attributions](#bb5f2bd7-1001-4dd8-8d7a-392e0ad90540)**
4. **[NPc](../../syntactic-functions/9/974ae899-afc0-41a9-ab2e-e418a95d76c9.md)** sentient being **[5 Attributions](#bb9a0e07-6410-4618-b6b1-bee654d05e08)**
5. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** *[singular](../../semantic-features/4/4e36ef0d-dcb2-48b8-a74a-daa9f2a54b2d.md)* a sentient being; any sentient being; just an ordinary sentient being **[3 Attributions](#ae86ba45-cc60-4b2e-beff-c76cc6fa3589)**
6. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** *[indefinite](../../semantic-features/c/c161d090-7e79-41e8-9615-93208fabbb99.md)* any sentient living creature; a sentient creature **[2 Attributions](#133fcdb6-bce9-437d-a7fb-5e377dc8f31f)**
