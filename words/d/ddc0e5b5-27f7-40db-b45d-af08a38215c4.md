  - Pinyin: jì
  - Old Chinese: kɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kɨ

## Definition
Jì 紀 refers to a cycle of 12 years, sān jì 三紀 refers to a period of thirty-six years, but from Later Han times, the reference is often more generally to a generation.
