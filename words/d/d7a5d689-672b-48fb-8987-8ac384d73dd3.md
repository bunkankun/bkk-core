  - Old Chinese: khljuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tɕhɨu

## Definition
Chǒu 醜 (ant. hǎo 好 "handsome") refers specifically to physical unsightliness and does not necessarily connote a general negative judgment on what is unsightly.
