  - Pinyin: fù
  - Old Chinese: bɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: bɨu

## Definition
Fù 負 occasionally refers to a defeat suffered, but usually in the combination shèng fù 勝負, and as a verb it does not take objects or complements. See FAIL
