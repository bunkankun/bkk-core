  - Pinyin: dōu móu
  - Old Chinese: too mu
  - Middle Chinese: tu mɨu

## Definition
Dōu móu 兜 鍪 is the term for a helmet which became common in the Han period. It probably refers mainly to the iron helmets; these were in Han times made of iron scales.
