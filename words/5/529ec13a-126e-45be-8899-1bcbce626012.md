  - Pinyin: yáo
  - Old Chinese: k-lew
  - Middle Chinese: jiɛu

## Definition
Yáo 謠 refers to a folksong, mostly in ancient texts to a children's ditty, with a more or less fixed melody, but the focus seems to be on the text and there is no accompanying music involved.
