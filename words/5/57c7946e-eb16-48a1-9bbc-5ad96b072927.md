## Definition
Tōu 偷 (ant. zhèng 正 "regularly and openly") refers to unreglemented inappropriate secrecy.

## Senses
1. **[vadV](../../syntactic-functions/2/2a0ded86-3b04-4488-bb7a-3efccfa35844.md)** furtively, in an unreglemented and irresponsible secretive way, secretly, unofficially；using underhand methods; in ways that do not tolerate the light of day **[14 Attributions](#157c2ec6-cc45-4237-b6b3-2836396d69a4)**
   - Usage: warring-states-currency: 3
