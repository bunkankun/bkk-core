# 戮: BEHEAD

## Form
- Orth: [戮](../../graphs/8/8dbe80f3-b063-4a20-980f-99947890e55c.md)
  - Pinyin: lù
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-ruɡ
  - Middle Chinese: luk

## Definition
Lù 戮 refers to beheading in addition to severe mutilation of the body and often involves beheading of the corpse.
