  - Old Chinese: tsuuŋ
  - Middle Chinese: tsuo̝ŋ

## Definition
Chóng 崇 and zōng 宗 refers to distant and typically religious veneration of a spirit or person very highly regarded. SHI 靡神不宗"there are no spirits which we have not honoured"
