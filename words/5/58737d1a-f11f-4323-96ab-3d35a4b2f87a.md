- Super-entry: [虛](../../super-entries/8/89e260c3-fcc2-4fd2-a260-c3fcc2bfd210.md)

## Form
- Orth: [虛](../../graphs/5/5dba505a-09f6-4697-b478-683963603e62.md)
  - Pinyin: xū
  - Old Chinese: qhla
  - Middle Chinese: hi̯ɤ

## Definition
The most geneal word is xū 虛 (ant. shí 實 "substantial and full") which can refer both to physical emptiness of a space and to abstract emptiness or tenuousness as a philosophical concept.
