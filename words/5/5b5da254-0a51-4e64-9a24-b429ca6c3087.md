- Orth: [德](../../graphs/9/954bd8cd-51ba-485f-b7f3-e5c5176e16c8.md)
  - Pinyin: dé
  - Old Chinese: tɯɯɡ
  - Middle Chinese: tək

## Definition
Dé 德 and the rarer zé 澤 refer to graceful and spiritually inspiring munificence, typically from a person of supremely high status.
