  - Old Chinese: lɯɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dəi

## Definition
Dài 怠 (ant. jìng 敬 "be respectfully dedicated to one's duties") refers to a customary failure to perform one's official duties with the appropriate seriousness of effort.
