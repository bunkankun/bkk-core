## Form
- Orth: [廣](../../graphs/3/3e0d32e6-429f-474d-bd76-acc4ffec7e7d.md)
  - Pinyin: guǎng
  - Old Chinese: kʷaaŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: kɑŋ

## Definition
The standard general word is guǎng 廣 (ant. xiá 狹 "narrow") referring to anything of get spatial extension, also in various metaphorical senses.
