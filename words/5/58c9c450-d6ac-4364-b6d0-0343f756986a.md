  - Pinyin: huì
  - Old Chinese: qhʷɯɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: huo̝i

## Definition
Huì 賄 and lù 賂 are words for "gift" which is often given with an ulterior purpose, and the word regularly comes to mean "bribe", especially in the current binome huì lù 賄賂 "bribe".
