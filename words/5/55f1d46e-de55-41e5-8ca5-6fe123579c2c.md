## Form
- Orth: [如](../../graphs/b/b70766fd-8fa3-4174-9134-d39d5f504d70.md)
  - Pinyin: rú
  - Old Chinese: nja
  - Middle Chinese: ȵi̯ɤ

## Definition
Rú 如 is often asymmetrical "be as good as> resemble" often adds to the notion of resemblance that of equipollence. [不如 always: "be not as good as"]
