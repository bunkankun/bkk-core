# 士: GENTLEMAN

- Super-entry: [士](../../super-entries/d/dd3212b8-ba74-4e20-b212-b8ba747e2080.md)
- Concept: [GENTLEMAN](../../concepts/3/36efaebe-58a9-4106-9ace-5dbcedf69de5.md)

## Form
- Orth: [士](../../graphs/f/fb89a673-a23b-40ad-ab82-7b44c4b3995e.md)
  - Pinyin: shì
  - Old Chinese: dzrɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ɖʐɨ

## Definition
The current general term for a person of a certain social standing is shì 士 and this word often connotes specifically a certain level of education, particularly literacy. See INTELLECTUAL.
