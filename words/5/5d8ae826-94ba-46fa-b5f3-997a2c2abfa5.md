## Form
- Orth: [節](../../graphs/7/74317e4c-51fa-4671-8feb-20c5313092bf.md)
  - Pinyin: jié
  - Old Chinese: tsiiɡ
  - Middle Chinese: tset

## Definition
Jié 節 (ant. sì 肆 "be unrestrained") always refers to abstract restraint and moderation, typically the restraint is directed towards oneself.
