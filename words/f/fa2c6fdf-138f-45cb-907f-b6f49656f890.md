# 偶: MATE

- Super-entry: [偶](../../super-entries/e/e7f1adb5-4803-4900-b1ad-b54803c900c4.md)
- Concept: [MATE](../../concepts/4/40d911ce-d453-41c0-b2a6-fe07e7b6c423.md)

## Form
- Orth: [偶](../../graphs/e/ed632a95-68b3-43a3-a07a-cf762f18e3da.md)
  - Pinyin: ǒu
  - Old Chinese: ŋooʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ŋu

## Definition
Ǒu 偶 refers to a concrete partner or to more abstractly, like Modern Chinese duìxiàng 對象 to refer to the notion of someone who will fill the role of partner of the other sex. NB: one may qiú ǒu 求偶 and xuǎn ǒu 選偶, but it appears pǐ 匹 is not used in such contexts.
