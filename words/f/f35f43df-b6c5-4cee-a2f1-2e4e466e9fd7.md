## Form
- Orth: [開](../../graphs/0/04eb6ef8-1900-411e-bfda-c184a22ed4a3.md)
  - Pinyin: kāi
  - Old Chinese: khɯɯl
  - Middle Chinese: khəi

## Definition
Kāi 開 (ant. bì 閉 "close") and pì 闢 "open wide" (ant. fēng 封 "close tightly") refer primarily to the opening of doors, windows and the like.
