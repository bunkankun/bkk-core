## Form

  - Pinyin: diàn
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-lɯɯmʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dem

## Definition
Diàn 簟 became common in Han times as a word referring to bamboo mats in general. The word appears already in SHIJING where it refers to a bamboo mat which can be used for various purposes, particularly to sleep upon. [SPECIFIC]; [[RARE]]
