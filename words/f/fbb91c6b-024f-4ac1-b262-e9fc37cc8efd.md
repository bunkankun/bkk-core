## Form
- Orth: [霾](../../graphs/f/f3b72b13-2b6a-4628-b1e4-4215d004f415.md)
  - Pinyin: mái
  - Old Chinese: mɢrɯɯ
  - Middle Chinese: mɣɛi

## Definition
Mái 霾 refers to a violent storm blowing as a sandstorm, or as a rainstorm.
