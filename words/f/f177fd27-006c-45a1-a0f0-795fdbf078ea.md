# 袍: UNDERWEAR

- Super-entry: [袍](../../super-entries/5/5f59f2cf-4b58-4201-99f2-cf4b58120153.md)

## Form
- Orth: [袍](../../graphs/2/2a768064-e899-4eec-aa6c-8eb56ed9f009.md)
  - Pinyin: páo
  - Old Chinese: buu
  - Middle Chinese: bɑu

## Definition
Páo 袍 refers to underwear in pre-Qin times, but this term includes what is worn above underwear but underneath the mean visible outside garment.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** SHI 133: dress worn between underwear and what is worn visibly on the outside (NB: from Han times the word referred to a main dress worn on the outside.)
