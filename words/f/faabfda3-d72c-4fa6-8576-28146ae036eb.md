## Form
- Orth: [時](../../graphs/e/e2aa15ab-5de1-4aef-9a8e-3d5313867d03.md)
  - Pinyin: shí
  - Old Chinese: ɡljɯ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑɨ

## Definition
The most current general term for a period of time is shí 時, but the term often retains its connotations of "seasons of the year".
