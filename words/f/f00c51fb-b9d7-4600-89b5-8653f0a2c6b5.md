## Definition
Rén 任 emphasises a general qualification to achieve something worth achieving and not obviously easy to achieve. ABSOLUTE, CURRENT, DIFFICULT, ENDOGENIC, ACQUIRED, TEMPORARY, PSYCHOLOGICAL.
