# 瞍: BLIND

- Super-entry: [瞍](../../super-entries/9/9cfb9097-896d-45b0-bb90-97896dd5b067.md)

## Form
- Orth: [瞍](../../graphs/b/bdabbb55-fe07-4b7f-b73c-62dfa4ef7936.md)
  - Pinyin: sǒu
  - Old Chinese: sooʔ
  - Middle Chinese: su

## Definition
Sǒu 瞍 refers ocasionally to blindness which is the result of atrophy or lack of eyeballs (as prototypically in the case of Music Master Kuàng 師曠), and this is an ancient elevated word to use.

## Senses
1. **[v\[adN\]](../../syntactic-functions/a/a7e8eabf-866e-42db-88f2-b8f753ab74be.md)** the blind; the dim-eyed **[2 Attributions](#e93b36ff-853f-4e8b-bc47-92ecbed31d85)**
2. **[vi](../../syntactic-functions/c/c20780b3-41f9-491b-bb61-a269c1c4b48f.md)** blindness as the result of atrophy of the eyeball, especially due to softening of the cornea during childhood due to vitamin deficiency
