## Form
- Orth: [謂](../../graphs/9/9990c2a1-0455-4bba-8bee-9ca94b7a97ce.md)
  - Pinyin: wèi
  - Old Chinese: ɢuds
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɨi

## Definition
Wèi 謂 refers to what a person or an expression is intended to refer to as an object or as the intended meaning, or to the semantic process itself.
