- Orth: [噓](../../graphs/5/529df314-aa12-4c0b-9946-f825c6763ed6.md)
  - Pinyin: xù
  - Old Chinese: qhlas
  - Middle Chinese: hi̯ɤ

## Definition
Xū 噓 refers to breathing lightly, in and out, without making any noise, but the focus is often on the breathing out rather than in.
