  - Old Chinese: qooɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ʔuk

## Definition
Wū 屋 refers originally to the roof, but from Han times onwards this word can come to replace gōng 宮 in its archaic meanings "family complex of buildings with a surrounding wall".
