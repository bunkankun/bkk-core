# 湎: SINK

- Super-entry: [湎](../../super-entries/a/a52c116e-8d6a-4dc2-ac11-6e8d6a5dc2ab.md)
- Concept: [SINK](../../concepts/f/fc2a61d1-7510-4952-855c-90799abe0179.md)

## Form
- Orth: [湎](../../graphs/f/f2cfadd3-8ac3-4f7f-a663-562637752f66.md)
  - Pinyin: miǎn
  - Old Chinese: menʔ
  - Middle Chinese: miɛn

## Definition
Miǎn 湎 "get immersed in" is mostly used in transferred senses such as "get immersed in wine" and thus gets close in meaning to dān 耽 "get addicted to".

## Senses
1. **[vtoN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fbfb2371-2537-4a99-a876-41b15ec2463c.md)** drown in wine; XUN, LIJI: drown in abstract things such as discussions etc
2. **[vttoN1+.vtoN2](../../syntactic-functions/c/cbb92823-4092-4552-8cbd-4883113a5422.md)** steep (something/somebody N1) in (something N2); drown something/somebody in
