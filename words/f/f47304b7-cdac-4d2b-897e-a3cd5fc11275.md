## Form
- Orth: [嬖](../../graphs/4/448d624a-8487-436d-bc55-f9d919334d88.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: peeɡs
  - Middle Chinese: pei

## Definition
Bì 嬖 refers to a male or female favourite of the political or sexual kind - or of both kinds.
