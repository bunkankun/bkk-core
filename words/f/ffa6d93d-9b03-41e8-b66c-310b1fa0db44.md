## Definition
Gōng 躬 (ant.* shǐ rén 使人 "get others to...") focusses on a person of considerable social status engaging personally in a (typically public) action. See SELF. [MARGINAL], [NOBLE], [OCCASIONAL], [PUBLIC!]
