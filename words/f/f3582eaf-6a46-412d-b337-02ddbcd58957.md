# 婦: WOMAN

- Super-entry: [婦](../../super-entries/0/0f69dfe2-e48b-4126-a9df-e2e48b31261d.md)
- Concept: [WOMAN](../../concepts/c/c96c706b-ce2d-49f3-abb0-73b84862c24e.md)

## Form
- Orth: [婦](../../graphs/e/ecdaa987-35be-48b0-82ce-acaf73c9a7e2.md)
  - Pinyin: fù
  - Old Chinese: buʔ
  - Middle Chinese: bɨu

## Definition
Fù 婦 (ant. fū 夫 "married man") refers to a woman who is either married or definitely of marriagable age.

## Senses

2. **[nadN](../../syntactic-functions/5/516d3836-3a0b-4fbc-b996-071cc48ba53d.md)** woman-like, for women (NB: in "the ritual practice of a woman" "woman" would not be nadN.) **[1 Attributions](#7b33f8fc-1eca-43f6-aab6-0464cd3ba7d9)**
