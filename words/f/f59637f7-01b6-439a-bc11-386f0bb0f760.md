## Form
- Orth: [偽](../../graphs/1/11aab8cc-d3c7-454c-8f8d-5714f315d3ed.md)
  - Pinyin: wěi
  - Old Chinese: ŋgʷals
  - Middle Chinese: ŋiɛ

## Definition
Wěi 偽 (ant. zhēn 真 "genuine") refers to the deliberate production of something artificial, and by extension the word comes to mean "pretend".
