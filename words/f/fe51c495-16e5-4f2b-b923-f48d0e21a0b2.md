1. **[NP](../../syntactic-functions/a/a8e89bab-49e1-4426-b230-0ec7887fd8b4.md)** *[buddhist](../../semantic-features/2/2e7204ae-4771-435b-82ff-310068296b6d.md)* BUDDH: skr. jambu-dvīpa; pali jambu-dīpa; 閻浮 transliterates jambu which originally refers to a kind of tree (Eugenia jambolana) and 提 refers to dvīpa, 'continent'; in Buddhist cosmology this refers to the southern continent among the four continents surrounding Mt.
