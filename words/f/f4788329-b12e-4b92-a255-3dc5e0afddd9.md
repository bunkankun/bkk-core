## Form
- Orth: [宵](../../graphs/3/3ec4886a-b1ed-449b-8cdc-819bdc87d900.md)
  - Pinyin: xiāo
  - Old Chinese: smew
  - Middle Chinese: siɛu

## Definition
Xiāo 宵 (ant. zhòu 晝) refers to the night only, without including the evening, and the reference is not to the length of time so much as to the absence of sunlight.
