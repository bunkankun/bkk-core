  - Pinyin: zhēng
  - Old Chinese: skreeŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈʂɣɛŋ

## Definition
The clearly dominant general word for competition or struggle, both hostile and friendly, is zhēng 爭 and the focus is on the conflict rather on what is being fought for. See STRUGGLE
