## Form
- Orth: [竊](../../graphs/3/35a3d12c-a3d3-4558-9a5b-48a4e62a29fe.md)
  - Pinyin: qiè
  - Old Chinese: tsheed
  - Middle Chinese: tshet

## Definition
The current general word for any form of stealing is qiè 竊 and this word in no way suggests that the person who steals is in fact a professional or habitual thief.
