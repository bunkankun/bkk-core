## Form

  - Pinyin: yù
  - Old Chinese: k-loɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ji̯ok

## Definition
Yù 欲 comes to be used as a neutral indicator of the likely future so that we are not likely to find bì yù 必欲 ever coming to mean "will surely, will necessarily".
