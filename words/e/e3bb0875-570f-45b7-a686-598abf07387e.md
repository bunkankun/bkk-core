## Form
- Orth: [然](../../graphs/8/8a15fd91-bd0f-4409-9544-18b3c2ea70d5.md)
  - Pinyin: rán
  - Old Chinese: njen
  - Middle Chinese: ȵiɛn

## Definition
The most current word for propositional truth is rán 然 "it is so" (ant. fǒu 否 "be untrue").
