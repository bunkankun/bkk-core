- Orth: [執](../../graphs/9/99ded5fd-627f-48cc-9764-8a1fe3728f61.md)
  - Pinyin: zhí
  - Old Chinese: tjib
  - Middle Chinese: tɕip

## Definition
Zhí 執 is often inchoative "take hold of", and then comes to mean "hold on to, keep holding" as one goes along.
