  - Old Chinese: paal
  - Middle Chinese: pʷɑ

## Definition
Bō 碆 refers originally to a stone placed on the ground to which one attached the stringed arrow (see illustrations). But the word is also used to refer to an arrowhead made of stone, synonymous with nǔ 砮.
