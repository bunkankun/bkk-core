# 去: DISTANT

- Super-entry: [去](../../super-entries/1/110b21f3-f22c-4e00-8b21-f3f22cde00e8.md)
- Concept: [DISTANT](../../concepts/2/2f82079e-afca-4f46-be9a-15d3981ed027.md)

## Form
- Orth: [去](../../graphs/8/827fc8a5-b76b-4a8f-b089-157ba660ab3f.md)
  - Pinyin: qù
  - Old Chinese: khas
  - Middle Chinese: khi̯ɤ

## Definition
Qù 去 can refer to concrete or abstract distance, and in the abstract case the distance is close in meaning to difference.
