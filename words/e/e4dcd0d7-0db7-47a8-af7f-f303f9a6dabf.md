# 雜: MIXED

## Form
- Orth: [雜](../../graphs/c/c9fba6b3-7c79-46b1-80aa-bad0aaf381ae.md)
  - Pinyin: zá
  - Old Chinese: sɡuub
  - Middle Chinese: dzəp

## Definition
The current word for anything not unified or mixed is zá 雜 (ant. cuì 粹"pure").
