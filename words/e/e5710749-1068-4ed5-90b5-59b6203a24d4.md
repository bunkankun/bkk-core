- Orth: [移](../../graphs/e/e18e2c3b-93b0-4203-80c7-01cb372cf3b4.md)
  - Pinyin: yí
  - Old Chinese: k-lal
  - Middle Chinese: jiɛ

## Definition
Yí 移 refers to dislocating anything to a new location where it comes to remain for some shorter or longer time.
