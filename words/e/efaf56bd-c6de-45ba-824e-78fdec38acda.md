## Form
- Orth: [什](../../graphs/c/c57ffddb-954e-4bdb-bccc-538ef824d5e4.md)
  - Pinyin: shí
  - Old Chinese: ɡjub
  - Middle Chinese: dʑip

## Definition
In the meaning "ten parts" both shí 十 and shí 什 are used. NB: In front of bèi 倍 one would expect shí 十, but shí bèi 什倍 is common and idiomatic.
