  - Pinyin: qǐng
  - Old Chinese: khʷleŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: khiɛŋ

## Definition
Qǐng 頃 refers to a short while, a certain reasonably short amount of time, always longer than xū yú 須臾"a moment".
