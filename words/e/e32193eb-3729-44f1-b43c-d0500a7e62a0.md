## Definition
Rán hòu 然後 (ant. yǐ qián 以前 "before") emphasises that an event occurs no sooner than after a certain event or space of time. See ONLY THEN. [BRIEF], [DRAMATIC], [EMPHATIC]; [PARTICLE]
