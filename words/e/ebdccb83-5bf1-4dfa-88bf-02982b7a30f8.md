## Form

  - Pinyin: qióng
  - Old Chinese: ɡʷɯŋ
  - Middle Chinese: guŋ

## Definition
Qióng 窮 (ant. dá 達 "successful") refers to getting into an impasse, a situation from which there is no way out, or being hopelessly unsuccessful with what one is trying to do.
