  - Pinyin: shǔ
  - Old Chinese: qhljaʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ɕi̯ɤ

## Definition
Shǔ 暑 (ant. hán 寒 "cold") refers to perceived heat, which often relates to humidity in the air as well as objective temperature.
