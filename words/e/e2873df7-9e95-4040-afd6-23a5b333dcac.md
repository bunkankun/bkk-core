## Form
- Orth: [謬](../../graphs/1/1cea464a-b0be-471f-84ab-0b2730a895da.md)
  - Pinyin: miù
  - Old Chinese: mɢrus
  - Middle Chinese: mi̯u

## Definition
Miù 謬 (ant. zhèng 正 "correct") typically refers to an intellectual misapprehension because of complete baselessness of what is being said.
