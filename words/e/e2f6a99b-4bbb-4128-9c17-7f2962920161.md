## Form
- Orth: [壞](../../graphs/4/4841b286-0d11-4064-85c6-0acd8c8f9ba8.md)
  - Pinyin: huài
  - Old Chinese: ɡruuls
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣɛi

## Definition
Huài 壞 and huī 墮/隳 are "to be destroyed, to collapse" but the words are also used transitively "cause to be destroyed, cause to collapse".
