# 眇: BLIND

- Super-entry: [眇](../../super-entries/e/ec4fb922-42d9-4f67-8fb9-2242d9af67c3.md)
- Concept: [BLIND](../../concepts/d/dcd7baf9-6457-4e34-ac1b-0f2a93a99019.md)

## Form
- Orth: [眇](../../graphs/2/2255862a-261c-4489-96ef-69c0efd98be0.md)
  - Pinyin: miǎo
  - Old Chinese: mewʔ
  - Middle Chinese: miɛu

## Definition
Miǎo 眇 refers to blindness on one eye because of near-sightnedness or strabismus; NB that in post-Han times the word came to refer to blindness on both eyes.
