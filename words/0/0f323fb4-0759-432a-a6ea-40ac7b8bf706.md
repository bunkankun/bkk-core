  - Pinyin: yào
  - Old Chinese: ŋɡraawɡs
  - Middle Chinese: ŋɣɛu

## Definition
The current general word for active enjoyment and delighting in something is lè 樂 (ant. bēi 悲 "be saddened by"), as in 與民同樂 "share one's enjoyings/enjoyments with the people". In the transitive usage the reading yào is suggested by WGZ.
