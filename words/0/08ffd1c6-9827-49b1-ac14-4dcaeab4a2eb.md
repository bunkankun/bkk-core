## Definition
Shàng 上 (ant. xià 下 "despise, have no special respect for") refer to giving high practical priority to something out of a sense of admiration or appreciation. [ASCENDING], [OBJ=NON-HU], [OBJECTIVE], [OVERT], [PUBLIC]
