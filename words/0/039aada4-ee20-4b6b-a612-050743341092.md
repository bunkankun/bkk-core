# 俎: CHOPPING BLOCK

- Super-entry: [俎](../../super-entries/5/595e639f-b8fd-4998-9e63-9fb8fd29986e.md)
- Concept: [CHOPPING BLOCK](../../concepts/2/2a790324-4787-4145-82dd-f309d53bfb83.md)

## Form
- Orth: [俎](../../graphs/c/c8c37521-02e6-4371-a79b-e2311b93c82e.md)
  - Pinyin: zǔ
  - Old Chinese: skraʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈʂi̯ɤ

## Definition
俎 zǔ refers to a small ritual table used for cutting fresh meat; it can also refer to a small chopping block on which the food is cut.

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** small elevated plate for chopping meat and other foods
