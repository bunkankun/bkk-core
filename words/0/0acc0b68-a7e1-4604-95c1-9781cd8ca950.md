## Form
- Orth: [道](../../graphs/0/012329d2-8a81-4a4f-ac3a-03885a49d6d6.md)
  - Pinyin: dào
  - Old Chinese: ɡ-luuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dɑu

## Definition
Dào 道 is a way of being, of functioning, as well as a way of doing things, and this Way may be either exoteric or esoteric.
