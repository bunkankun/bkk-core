- Super-entry: [慎](../../super-entries/d/db007353-c772-4ae0-8073-53c772bae039.md)

## Form
- Orth: [慎](../../graphs/e/eaf40a23-c1b4-4cdb-8246-c1dc0b2adb48.md)
  - Pinyin: shèn
  - Old Chinese: djins
  - Middle Chinese: dʑin

## Definition
Shèn 慎 (ant. hū 忽 "be neglectful") "be careful and cautious" emphasises not only careful attention but also wariness of possible danger.
