# 歲: YEAR

## Form
- Orth: [歲](../../graphs/b/bd29e351-3cf9-47e0-a8d7-ae7062fd81c8.md)
  - Pinyin: suì
  - Old Chinese: sqʷads
  - Middle Chinese: siɛi

## Definition
Suì 歲 , named after the star Juppiter, can refer to years in general, but after a number word suì 歲 typically refers to years of age and not to number of years as such. This term is astronomical in origin and defines the year primarily in terms of the revolutions of the planets.
