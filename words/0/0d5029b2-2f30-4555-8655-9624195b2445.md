- Orth: [禱](../../graphs/b/b519c877-6934-4158-b984-1b71a6c734ac.md)
  - Pinyin: dǎo
  - Old Chinese: tuuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tɑu

## Definition
The current general word for prayers of all kinds is dǎo 禱, but the word primarily refers to requests made to the spirits.
