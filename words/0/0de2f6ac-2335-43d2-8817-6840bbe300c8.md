# 皇: KING

- Super-entry: [皇](../../super-entries/d/d607a6b9-6cf0-41b4-87a6-b96cf061b467.md)
- Concept: [KING](../../concepts/e/eb501015-384f-45b3-9603-06fd2f743e8b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [皇](../../graphs/d/d9c056c5-eb3d-4ac0-a0aa-be11ca2c1976.md)
  - Pinyin: huáng
  - Old Chinese: ɡʷaaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɑŋ

## Definition
Huáng 皇 is a very archaic way of referring to mythical rulers which in Qin times came to be used as part of the term for "emperor".

## Senses
1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** from Qín times; gāo huáng 高皇 "high emperor"
