- Super-entry: [悲](../../super-entries/0/0e0caa07-8293-4a10-8caa-078293da103f.md)

## Form
- Orth: [悲](../../graphs/8/892c7455-3307-45ca-a2d2-ca585c175e4d.md)
  - Pinyin: bēi
  - Old Chinese: prɯl
  - Middle Chinese: pi

## Definition
The general word for sadness of any kind is bēi 悲 (ant. huān 歡 "joyful" and xǐ 喜 "delighted").
