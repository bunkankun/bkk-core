  - Pinyin: gǔ
  - Old Chinese: kooɡ
  - Middle Chinese: kuk

## Definition
Gǔ 穀 refers specifically to salary in the form of grain, which was the most current form of payment, and the word sometimes refers in a generalised way to salaries of any kind.
