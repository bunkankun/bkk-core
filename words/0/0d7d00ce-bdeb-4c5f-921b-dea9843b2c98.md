  - Pinyin: shòu
  - Old Chinese: djuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑɨu

## Definition
The most general word referring to a person's being exposed to something is probably shòu 受 (ant. shī 施 "have an effect on, act upon"), which can take both desirable and non-desirable objects.
