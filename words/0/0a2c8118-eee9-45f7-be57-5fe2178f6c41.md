# 衣: WEAR

- Super-entry: [衣](../../super-entries/5/5107a993-3ae4-4b28-87a9-933ae4bb282d.md)

## Form
- Orth: [衣](../../graphs/a/a1b9925f-9205-4ac2-9a62-cc2926a20cb9.md)
  - Pinyin: yì
  - Old Chinese: qɯls
  - Middle Chinese: ʔɨi

## Definition
The current general word for wearing clothes or putting on clothes of any kind is yì 衣 (ant. tuō 脫 "take off").
