## Form
- Orth: [厚](../../graphs/c/c7a734bf-a4f4-4a9f-86fe-286898376f9c.md)
  - Pinyin: hòu
  - Old Chinese: ɡoos
  - Middle Chinese: ɦu

## Definition
Hòu 厚 (ant. báo 薄 "less than ample") refers to generosity of treatment which is not necessarily directed towards inferiors.
