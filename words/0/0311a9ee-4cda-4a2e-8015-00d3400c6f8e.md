## Form

  - Pinyin: niǔ
  - Old Chinese: m-luʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ɳɨu

## Definition
Niǔ 狃 and xiè 褻 (ant. jìng 敬"show proper respect to"(!)) refers to excessive and reprehensible and often even indecent overfamiliarity.
