# 陛: STAIRS

- Super-entry: [陛](../../super-entries/a/ab4f8366-078c-4434-8f83-66078c343401.md)
- Concept: [STAIRS](../../concepts/0/07178f82-5672-4502-9797-85c0c58d141b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [陛](../../graphs/9/9c200665-faf0-4c10-a92d-2033ca6a3c3d.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: biiʔ
  - Middle Chinese: bei

## Definition
Bì 陛 is another word for stairs and it refers to the high stairs. Since Qin times, the word specifically referred to the stairs used by the emperor in his palace.
