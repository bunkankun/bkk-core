## Form
- Orth: [忍](../../graphs/c/c24285cf-ab7c-4a03-b4a2-be5e3575cef6.md)
  - Pinyin: rěn
  - Old Chinese: njinʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ȵin

## Definition
Rěn 忍 refers to any callous ability to bear with something that is objectionable without interfering to stop it.
