- Orth: [陷](../../graphs/3/32a5e3a6-a0ed-47aa-b025-dee63abaaedb.md)
  - Pinyin: xiàn
  - Old Chinese: ɡrooms
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣɛm

## Definition
Xiàn 陷 refers to falling into objects like pits or holes in the ground, also bogs zé 澤, but not rivers and the like.
