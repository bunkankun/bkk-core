- Orth: [聽](../../graphs/0/09c04962-078d-47a0-b24e-33d4565e5c40.md)
  - Pinyin: tīng
  - Old Chinese: theeŋ
  - Middle Chinese: theŋ

## Definition
The current word for listening is tīng 聽, the nuance often being listening to and at the same time taking note of as sound advice etc.
