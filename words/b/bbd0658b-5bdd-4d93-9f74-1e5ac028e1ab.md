## Definition
Wěi 唯(ant. fǒu 否 "refuse") expresses an unquestioning agreement directed at superiors to do what is asked of one.concerning a major undertaking by means of a mutual promise. [ASCENDING], [EXPLICIT], [FORMAL], [REACTIVE]
