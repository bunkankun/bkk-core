## Definition
Tǐng 艇 refers to the boat even smaller than the previous, with space for one or two people. According to HUAINANZI, this was originally used in the area of Sichuan. [HUANG 1995: 1340; ill.: HAYASHI 1976: pp. 150, tab. 7-74 - 7-76???]
