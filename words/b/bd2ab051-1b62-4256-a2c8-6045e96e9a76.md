  - Old Chinese: ɡjenʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑiɛn

## Definition
Měi 美 and shàn 善 (all ant. wù 惡 "speak ill of") refer to the bringing out of positive, admirable or morally commendable aspects in something or someone through discourse.
