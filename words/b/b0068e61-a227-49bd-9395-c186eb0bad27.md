- Orth: [智](../../graphs/3/3cb5236a-c2dc-42a6-92ba-89e6f7a43e85.md)
  - Pinyin: zhì
  - Old Chinese: tes
  - Middle Chinese: ʈiɛ

## Definition
The most general word for craftiness as a negative quality is zhì 智, although very confusingly, this word can also refer to positive wisdom as a virtue.
