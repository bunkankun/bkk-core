## Definition
Àn 黯 (ant. hào 昊"shining bright (of sky)") is rare and poetic, and the word refers to the threatening darkness of clouds in a thunderstorm. [DRAMATIC], [ELEVATED], [LITERAL], [POETIC]; [[RARE]]
