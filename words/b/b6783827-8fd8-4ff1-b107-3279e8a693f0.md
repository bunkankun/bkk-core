  - Pinyin: gēng
  - Old Chinese: kraaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: kɣaŋ

## Definition
Gēng 更 is an act of deliberately changing something, typically by replacing it by an improved version of the same kind of thing in order to insure continuity.
