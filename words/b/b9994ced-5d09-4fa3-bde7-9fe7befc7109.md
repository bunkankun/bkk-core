# 止: STOP

- Super-entry: [止](../../super-entries/0/005bcaa9-0c56-4fa9-9bca-a90c56bfa96c.md)
- Concept: [STOP](../../concepts/6/6f413584-adaf-4fe7-b0e9-c9b428555e40.md)

## Form
- Orth: [止](../../graphs/6/6556964e-355c-4f58-93fa-31077a01ad93.md)
  - Pinyin: zhǐ
  - Old Chinese: kljɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: tɕɨ

## Definition
The standard current word for ceasing in an activity is zhǐ 止 (ant. xíng 行 "carry on with"), and this word can to any concrete or abstract activity; the ceasing may be final or temporary.
