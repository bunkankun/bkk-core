## Senses
1. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** draft (or outline) character form, in opposition to kǎishū 楷書. Just as 楷, 草 did not originally refer to a specific character form, so each character form has its corresponding cǎo 草 character form (章草/小篆; 今草/楷書).
