## Form
- Orth: [穢](../../graphs/9/94beed8f-0b65-4bc1-ba27-6b3b2c177f48.md)
  - Pinyin: huì
  - Old Chinese: qʷads
  - Middle Chinese: ʔi̯ɐi

## Definition
Huì 穢 can refer to physical dirt, but the word is also used abstractly to refer to what is vulgar and morally distasteful.
