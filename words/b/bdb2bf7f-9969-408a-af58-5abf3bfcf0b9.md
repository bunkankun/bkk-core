  - Old Chinese: daaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: dɑŋ

## Definition
Táng 堂 refers to the hall which seems to serve mainly ceremonial and since the Warring States also political purposes. From Han times táng 堂 in its original meaning was replaced by diàn 殿.
