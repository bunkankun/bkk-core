# 小: UNIMPORTANT

- Super-entry: [小](../../super-entries/f/f2ef5ca3-5211-4b1a-af5c-a35211db1a26.md)

## Form
- Orth: [小](../../graphs/8/83c7a7f5-03b1-4bfd-b668-386b60478132.md)
  - Pinyin: xiǎo
  - Old Chinese: smewʔ
  - Middle Chinese: siɛu

## Definition
Xiǎo 小 (ant. dà 大 "of great importance") is sometimes used as a colourless term denying the importance of something.
