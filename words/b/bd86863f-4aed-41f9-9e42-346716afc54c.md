# 瑗: RING

- Super-entry: [瑗](../../super-entries/2/228afc54-e669-409b-8afc-54e669609b91.md)
- Concept: [RING](../../concepts/3/362ff7ea-a564-430d-a3f0-a3b355fdd85a.md)

## Form
- Orth: [瑗](../../graphs/f/f0749933-c03a-4aa7-8b5f-64e9d00b698c.md)
  - Pinyin: yuàn
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷans
  - Middle Chinese: ɦiɛn

## Definition
Yuàn 瑗 is another kind of a circular jade pendant used in the same way like preceding two. According to SHUOWEN, its breadth should be smaller than the radius of the central hole.
