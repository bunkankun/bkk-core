## Form
- Orth: [平](../../graphs/c/c9cae2f5-ed2c-4c67-afd6-bbdcacee076f.md)
  - Pinyin: píng
  - Old Chinese: breŋ
  - Middle Chinese: bɣaŋ

## Definition
Píng 平 (ant. luàn 亂 "chaos") is prototypically an unruffled smooth surface, and the word refers to a state of harmonious tranquility, particularly in a community of people.
