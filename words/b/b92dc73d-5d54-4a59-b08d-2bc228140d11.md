  - Old Chinese: srɯʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʂɨ

## Definition
Shǐ 使 (ant. jìn 禁 "prohibit, forbid") is sometimes used to refer to a failure to prevent someone from doing something which amounts to an implicit form of permission.
