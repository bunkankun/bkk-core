## Form
- Orth: [亦](../../graphs/6/6572f778-b88e-4170-9f05-3eda652316f7.md)
  - Pinyin: yì
  - Old Chinese: k-laɡ
  - Middle Chinese: jiɛk

## Definition
Yì 亦 and bù yì 不亦 are both very different from bì 必 in that they do not assert necessity but only indubitable factual truth.
