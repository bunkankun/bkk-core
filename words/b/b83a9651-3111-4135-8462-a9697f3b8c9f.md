  - Pinyin: yè
  - Old Chinese: qad
  - Middle Chinese: ʔi̯ɐt

## Definition
Yè 謁 refers to (inappropriate) special requests made to acquaintances in high office, and the term can come to have negative overtones when used as a noun.
