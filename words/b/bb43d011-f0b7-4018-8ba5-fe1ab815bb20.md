  - Pinyin: biàn
  - Old Chinese: brons
  - Middle Chinese: biɛn

## Definition
Biàn 弁 refers to the ceremonial hats worn as signs of their dignity, on lesser occasions, by high officials including the emperor himself, and often this is specified as a pí biàn 皮弁.
