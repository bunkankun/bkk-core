  - Pinyin: píng
  - Old Chinese: breŋ
  - Middle Chinese: bɣaŋ

## Definition
The general word for flatness of any kind is píng 平 (ant. qīng 傾 "slanted downwards on one side" and perhaps xié 斜 "sloping") and it refers to anything which is horizontal and lacks inclination.
