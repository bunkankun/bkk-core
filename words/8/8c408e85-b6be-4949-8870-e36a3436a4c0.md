  - Old Chinese: ɢʷiids
  - Middle Chinese: ɦei

## Definition
Probably the most general word for generosity is huì 惠 (ant. sè 嗇 "stingy, ungenerous"), and this refers to any kind of emotional as well as material munificence by a person of superior status.
