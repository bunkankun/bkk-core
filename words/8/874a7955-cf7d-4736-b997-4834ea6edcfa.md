# 塞: BLOCK

## Form
- Orth: [塞](../../graphs/c/c5c54cf7-ac80-4e48-a788-423225e9e3d1.md)
  - Pinyin: sāi
  - Old Chinese: sɯɯɡ
  - Middle Chinese: sək

## Definition
Sè 塞 (ant. tōng 通 "be unblocked") is the standard word for blocking the progress of something, or for blocking up an opening, and the word specifically refers to the blocking of things from getting out of something. [GENERAL]
