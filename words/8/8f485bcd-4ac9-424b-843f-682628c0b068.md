## Form
- Orth: [翁](../../graphs/9/971eaa6d-4971-4f1b-b232-1dfa290db370.md)
  - Pinyin: wēng
  - Old Chinese: qlooŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ʔuŋ

## Definition
Wēng 翁 "old man" is occasionally used, from Han times onwards, to refer periphrastically to one's aged father.
