  - Pinyin: què, quē
  - Old Chinese: khod
  - Middle Chinese: khi̯ɐt

## Definition
Quē 闕 can refer specifically to the deliberate or inadvertent omission of what one might expect was there, but sometimes the word is used interchangeably with quē 缺.
