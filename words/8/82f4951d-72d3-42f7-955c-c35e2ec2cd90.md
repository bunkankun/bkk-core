## Form
- Orth: [具](../../graphs/a/aa2a7159-1647-43b5-aa68-7568d264d84c.md)
  - Pinyin: jù
  - Old Chinese: ɡos
  - Middle Chinese: gi̯o

## Definition
Jù 具 refers to providing something or assuring supplies of something without much emphasis on what these things are to be used for in concrete terms.
