# 是: COPULA

- Super-entry: [是](../../super-entries/b/b85d54ed-23f1-44ee-9d54-ed23f1e4ee44.md)
- Concept: [COPULA](../../concepts/4/418ea48e-b91f-4303-8076-be3ca87f9da5.md)

## Form
- Orth: [是](../../graphs/4/4342b9fe-7e09-40cb-ad1a-fbf479505d5f.md)
  - Pinyin: shì
  - Old Chinese: ɡljeʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑiɛ

## Definition
Shì 是 is a resumptive demonstrative pronoun which on very rare occasions can come to function very much as a copula.
