  - Pinyin: xí
  - Old Chinese: ɡeeɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ɦek

## Definition
Xí 覡 is a rare word which seems to have been limited to male shamans in pre-Han times but came to be used generally for shamanes female and male alike, in post-Han times.
