## Form
- Orth: [眩](../../graphs/1/1644df48-75f0-4842-acd6-4ecdb1c97bb5.md)
  - Pinyin: xuàn
  - Old Chinese: ɡʷeens
  - Middle Chinese: ɦen

## Definition
Xuàn 眩 refers to non-congenital blindness, but the word is very often used metaphorically in the sense of "dazzled".
