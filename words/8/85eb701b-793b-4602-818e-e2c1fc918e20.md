# 蔽: STUPID

- Super-entry: [蔽](../../super-entries/3/36eeebbe-b2e3-466d-aeeb-beb2e3c66d7a.md)

## Form
- Orth: [蔽](../../graphs/2/29f16dca-c69b-4e8c-aa1f-981e38a879b4.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: peds
  - Middle Chinese: piɛi

## Definition
Bì 蔽 and měng 蒙 (all ant. cōng 聰 "clever") , refer to an appearance of stupidity due to limited access to information. a state of stupidity that is typically construed as remediable.
