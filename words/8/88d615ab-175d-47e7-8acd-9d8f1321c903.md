  - Middle Chinese: ɕi

## Definition
The general dignified term for a dead body is shī 尸/屍 often in connection with burial.

1. **[n](../../syntactic-functions/8/8717712d-14a4-4ae2-be7a-6e18e61d929b.md)** 屍 is a variant character created to refer unambiguously to the corpse rather than the impersonator of the dead also written as 尸 **[5 Attributions](#08ab4678-6330-495a-9b12-9cfe7783fe76)**
