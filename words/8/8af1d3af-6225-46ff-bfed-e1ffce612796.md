## Form
- Orth: [下](../../graphs/2/28f7e200-9ed0-458d-9c74-cd4dd9f6cf9f.md)
  - Pinyin: xià
  - Old Chinese: ɢraas
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣɛ

## Definition
Xià 下 (ant. shàng 上 "ascend") sometimes refers colourlessly to something moving downwards.
