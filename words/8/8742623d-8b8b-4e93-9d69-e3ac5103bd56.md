- Orth: [受](../../graphs/7/7956102e-4f68-4cd7-b24c-33aed9e56072.md)
  - Pinyin: shòu
  - Old Chinese: djuʔ
  - Middle Chinese: dʑɨu

## Definition
The general word for accepting something offered to one is shòu 受, but the distinction with the passive "receive" is not made explicit. >>NEUTRAL
