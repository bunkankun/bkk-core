- Orth: [將](../../graphs/6/69629cac-c2c1-4e4e-973b-f5d11b631144.md)
  - Pinyin: jiāng
  - Old Chinese: skaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: tsi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
The current general word for the immediate future is jiāng 將 (ant. yǐ 已 "already") which refers freely to any part of the future, distant or imminent.
