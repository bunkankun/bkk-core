# 崇: HIGH

- Super-entry: [崇](../../super-entries/0/0e48597b-70b6-4e5d-8859-7b70b6be5df0.md)

## Form
- Orth: [崇](../../graphs/c/c9067ebb-d389-463d-8da6-58f0fd15f6b5.md)
  - Pinyin: chóng
  - Old Chinese: dzruŋ
  - Middle Chinese: ɖʐuŋ

## Definition
Chóng 崇 (ant. bēi 卑 "lowly") and lóng 隆 (ant. wū 污) adds the notion of impressiveness to that of high elevation.
