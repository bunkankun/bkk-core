## Definition
The current standard word for an idea in someone's mind is yì 意.

## Senses
1. **[nab](../../syntactic-functions/7/76be1df4-3d73-4e5f-bbc2-729542645bc8.md)** *[mathematical term](../../semantic-features/b/b110bae1-02d5-4c66-ad13-7c04b3ee3ad9.md)* CHEMLA 2003: refers to the reasoning or "idea" behind a mathematical procedure.Mathematical commentarial practice crucially involved not only the reconstruction of effective procedures (which would make Chinese mathematics into a mere art of calculation devoid of theoretical basis) but also the underlying 意 "idea/reasoning behind" the procedure. Thus in his introduction to JZ Liu Hui ejaculates: 總算術之根源，探賾之暇，遂悟其意 "I got an overall view of the underlying principles of the (various) procedures of mathematics, and while taking a rest from the exploration of these mysteries, in the end, in a sudden moment of insight, I got their basic ideas/the underlying reasonings!".Liu Hui writes in his postface about 古人之意 "the underlying idea of the ancients (i.e. concerning the 重差 procedure)", thus there is a historical as well as a systematic motivation behind his quest for the underlying idea.This historical underlying reasoning/idea may be ancient or not, but in any case it may be wrong: JZ, Liu Hui's comm 4.24: 此意非也 "this reasoning is mistaken". **[1 Attributions](#f9654ae1-e79d-46b9-9735-9a3713ad67af)**
