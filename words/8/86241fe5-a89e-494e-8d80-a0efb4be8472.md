- Orth: [徙](../../graphs/5/59f0d1ee-d681-4464-afc9-902eeb1a01ce.md)
  - Pinyin: xǐ
  - Old Chinese: selʔ
  - Middle Chinese: siɛ

## Definition
Xǐ 徙 (ant. liú 留 "stay put") refers to moving permanently, often but not necessarily over a long distance.
