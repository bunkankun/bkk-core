# 失: MISTAKE

- Super-entry: [失](../../super-entries/4/4bfa1acf-1dbc-4a5b-ba1a-cf1dbc1a5bca.md)
- Concept: [MISTAKE](../../concepts/0/0afde884-7a1d-43f1-9b2c-69fedf653c8c.md)

## Form
- Orth: [失](../../graphs/d/df3b2343-918c-4300-911b-9206b25c5d01.md)
  - Pinyin: shī
  - Old Chinese: lʰiɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ɕit

## Definition
The most current general word for a mistake is probably shī 失 (ant. dé 得 "get things right") refers generally to an inadvertent mistake in action, and normally of minor kind.
