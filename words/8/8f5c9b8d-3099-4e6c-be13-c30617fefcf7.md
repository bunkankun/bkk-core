## Form
- Orth: [小](../../graphs/8/83c7a7f5-03b1-4bfd-b668-386b60478132.md)[人](../../graphs/2/21fa0930-1ebd-4609-9c0d-ef7ef7a2723f.md)
  - Pinyin: xiǎo rén
  - Old Chinese: smewʔ njin
  - Middle Chinese: siɛu ȵin

## Definition
Xiǎo rén 小人 (ant. jūn zǐ 君子) is a term of moral or personal appraisal and refers to a person of minor worth or of no moral merit.
