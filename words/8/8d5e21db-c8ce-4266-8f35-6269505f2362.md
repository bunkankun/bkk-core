  - Pinyin: cè
  - Old Chinese: skhreeɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ʈʂhɣɛk

## Definition
Cè 策 refers specifically to a bamboo whip which remained current in Han times. The cè 策 has always been used to drive horses, but seems to have gone out of use in late Han times.
