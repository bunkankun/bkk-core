  - Old Chinese: smaaŋ
  - Middle Chinese: sɑŋ

## Definition
The general term for all forms of mourning dress, including the garments themselves and everything worn on them, was sāng 喪, and this term designates such articles of clothing insofar as they are used for their purpose.
