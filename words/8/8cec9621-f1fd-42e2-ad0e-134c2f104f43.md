# 愧: SHAME

- Super-entry: [愧](../../super-entries/e/ec9b26d5-cb1a-48ba-9b26-d5cb1a08ba83.md)
- Concept: [SHAME](../../concepts/c/cd09cddd-d12d-4847-ad72-e3f9eec74920.md)

## Form
- Orth: [愧](../../graphs/4/4fc10c08-ec15-4dd6-b920-96331a0ec7aa.md)
  - Pinyin: kuì
  - Old Chinese: kruls
  - Middle Chinese: ki

## Definition
Kuì 愧 expresses a mixture of shame and embarrassment, and the word is the only shame-word to be used as a reflexive verb meaning "be ashamed of and embarrassed about oneself".
