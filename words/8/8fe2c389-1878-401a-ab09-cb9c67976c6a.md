# 弱: WEAK

- Super-entry: [弱](../../super-entries/a/a4cd1890-7150-40d4-8d18-907150f0d49d.md)

## Form
- Orth: [弱](../../graphs/4/4d9ad900-b9f4-4297-808a-ac1b45292f9c.md)
  - Pinyin: ruò
  - Old Chinese: njewɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ȵi̯ɐk

## Definition
The current general word for weakness of any physical or abstract kind, and of anything including states, persons, and animals is ruò 弱 (ant. qiáng 強 "strong").
