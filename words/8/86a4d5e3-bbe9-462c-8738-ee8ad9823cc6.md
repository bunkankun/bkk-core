## Form
- Orth: [年](../../graphs/8/8bf08783-3163-4314-b7a0-a12b96bd9b07.md)
  - Pinyin: nián
  - Old Chinese: niin
  - Middle Chinese: nen

## Definition
The most current general word for the year is the basically agricultural nián 年. This word can refer to the years of age as well as to the time-span of a year as such. After a number word nián 年 always refers to a number of years as such and not to a person's age.
