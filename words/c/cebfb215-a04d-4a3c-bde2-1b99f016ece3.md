## Form
- Orth: [夕](../../graphs/8/896037ed-8e31-43f6-af56-4758697caa68.md)
  - Pinyin: xī
  - Old Chinese: sɢlaɡ
  - Middle Chinese: ziɛk

## Definition
The current general word for the evening is xì 夕 (ant. zhāo 朝 "morning"), and the term refers to the period when the sun is declining and including the time when the sun has just gone down.
