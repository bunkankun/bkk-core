  - Pinyin: yǒng
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷaŋs
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɣaŋ

## Definition
The current word for chanting prose for the benefit of others is sòng 誦, and the prose chanted normally has to be of high cultural status.
