# 沒: SINK

- Super-entry: [沒](../../super-entries/4/4037a0f2-410c-4392-b7a0-f2410c139265.md)
- Concept: [SINK](../../concepts/f/fc2a61d1-7510-4952-855c-90799abe0179.md)

## Form
- Orth: [沒](../../graphs/5/5630f3f0-6ce2-4e2e-a99f-c7284bf35660.md)
  - Pinyin: mò
  - Old Chinese: mɯɯd
  - Middle Chinese: muot

## Definition
Mò 沒"get submerged" (ant. piāo 漂 "float") expresses the result of chén 沈 "sinking".

## Senses
1. **[vadN](../../syntactic-functions/f/fed035db-e7bd-4d23-bd05-9698b26e38f9.md)** submerged in water; diving (沒人 diver)
