  - Pinyin: shàng
  - Old Chinese: ɡljaŋs
  - Middle Chinese: dʑi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Shàng 上(ant. xià 下 "lower") refers to the relatively high position of something, both abstractly and concretely.
