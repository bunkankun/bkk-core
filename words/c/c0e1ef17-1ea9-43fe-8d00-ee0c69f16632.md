# 孽: DESCENDANT

## Form
- Orth: [孽](../../graphs/0/08cb8fde-3583-4630-89a1-96889f3be3e4.md)
  - Pinyin: niè
  - Old Chinese: ŋred
  - Middle Chinese: ŋiɛt

## Definition
Shù 庶 and niè 孽 (ant. zhèng 正 "descendant in direct line") refer to offspring by other women than the main wife.
