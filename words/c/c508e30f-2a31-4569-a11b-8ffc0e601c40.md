  - Pinyin: sì líng
  - Old Chinese: plids reeŋ
  - Middle Chinese: si leŋ

## Definition
Sìlíng 四靈 is a general term for four supernational creations: dragon lóng 龍, tiger hǔ 虎, phoenix fènghuáng 風凰, and turtle guī 龜.
