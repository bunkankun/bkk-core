# 壁: CITY WALL

- Super-entry: [壁](../../super-entries/3/3582f2a7-d2d7-48a2-82f2-a7d2d7b8a245.md)
- Concept: [CITY WALL](../../concepts/0/097ce0e4-819b-489f-b034-a9d4906938e1.md)

## Form
- Orth: [壁](../../graphs/b/b5de29bb-f1be-45c3-981f-c3d1ff3ca1b9.md)
  - Pinyin: bì
  - Old Chinese: peeɡ
  - Middle Chinese: pek

## Definition
Bì 壁 refers to the unstable earthen wall of certain height built around military encampment. The term began to be used in the Warring States period.
