  - Pinyin: wáng
  - Old Chinese: maŋ
  - Middle Chinese: mi̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Wáng 亡 and the much rarer sàng 喪 often refers to a dignified almost public way of leaving one place for another, especially to going into exile in order to escape danger.
