## Form
- Orth: [後](../../graphs/7/79ba8c80-7f2a-411d-9323-2249801433ea.md)
  - Pinyin: hòu
  - Old Chinese: ɡooʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ɦu

## Definition
The current general word for lateness is hòu 後 (ant. xiān 先 "earlier"), and this word refers to anything that comes later in time than something else.
