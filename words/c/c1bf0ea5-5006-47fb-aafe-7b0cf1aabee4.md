  - Pinyin: xiǎng
  - Old Chinese: sqaŋʔ
  - Middle Chinese: si̯ɐŋ

## Definition
Xiǎng 想 is rare in pre-Buddhist Chinese and tends to refer to thinking of someone not only with affection but also with anticipation and hope for future contact.
