# 美: EXCELLENT

- Super-entry: [美](../../super-entries/0/01bb281d-d469-4690-bb28-1dd469569006.md)

## Form
- Orth: [美](../../graphs/f/f05378e7-1d93-40cf-9fe8-2b8d58428fa2.md)
  - Pinyin: měi
  - Old Chinese: mriʔ
  - Middle Chinese: mi

## Definition
Měi 美 (ant. è 惡 "of bad quality") when it does not have its standard meaning "beauty" indicates striking or admirable high quality of anything concrete or abstract.
