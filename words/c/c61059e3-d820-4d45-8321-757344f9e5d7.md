- Super-entry: [爰](../../super-entries/f/fd0b5006-54a4-485d-8b50-0654a4085d7b.md)

## Form
- Orth: [爰](../../graphs/8/8aa6fc4f-a5d1-4e2f-976c-dcc761a32a3f.md)
  - Pinyin: yuán
  - Old Chinese: ɢʷan
  - Middle Chinese: ɦi̯ɐn

## Definition
Yuán 爰 refers to the rectangular pieces of gold which were used like money in the state of Chu. It is the earliest golden currency in China.
