# 咬: CHEW

- Super-entry: [咬](../../super-entries/e/ea2024a1-dd08-4c0e-a024-a1dd08bc0e5f.md)
- Concept: [CHEW](../../concepts/a/abf865bb-e7e8-4285-b98b-adda734fe635.md)

## Form
- Orth: [咬](../../graphs/1/191634b4-4739-4af5-bd87-4a529d71b2a5.md)
  - Pinyin: yǎo
  - Old Chinese: ŋɡreewʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ŋɣɛu

## Definition
Yǎo 咬 merely refers to the biting into something, and secondarily the chewing over of it, but the word never refers to swallowing.
