## Definition
Yì 翌 is the ancient OBI word for a period of time that is imminent, and the word always retained a literary, elevated, and even antiquated flavour.

## Senses
1. **[nadN](../../syntactic-functions/5/516d3836-3a0b-4fbc-b996-071cc48ba53d.md)** OBI: upcoming within the present decameron but not immediately next
