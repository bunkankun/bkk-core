## Form
- Orth: [苛](../../graphs/0/032db148-5c9a-4c28-9f66-ffad2da391c7.md)
  - Pinyin: kē
  - Old Chinese: ɡlaal
  - Middle Chinese: ɦɑ

## Definition
Kē 苛 and kù 酷 (ant.* rén 仁 "kind-hearted" and bù rěn 不忍 "be unable to stand") refer to flagrant extravagance in the severeness of punishments.
