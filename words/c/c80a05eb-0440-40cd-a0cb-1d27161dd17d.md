## Definition
Yǒu jiān 有間 (ant.* xuǎn 旋／還 "without delay", dùn 頓 "immediately, without hesitation") refers to a brief interval of a certain time which ensues, and after which something new happens in the narrative sequence. [BRIEF], [UNDRAMATIC], [UNEMPHATIC]; [CLAUSE]
