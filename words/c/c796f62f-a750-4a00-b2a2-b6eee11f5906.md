## Definition
Cóng 從 "do as one is told" (ant. cí 辭 "refuse") can refer to an agreement by a superior to do as is being suggested by an inferior rather than the obeying itself. See OBEY [IMPLICIT], [PRACTICAL], [REACTIVE]
