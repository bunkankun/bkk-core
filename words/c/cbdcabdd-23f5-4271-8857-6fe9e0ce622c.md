## Form
- Orth: [夭](../../graphs/1/110b1ad2-37c6-4af7-bb13-d93535338b32.md)
  - Pinyin: yāo
  - Old Chinese: qrowʔ
  - Middle Chinese: ʔiɛu

## Definition
Yāo (old: yǎo) 夭 , yǎo 殀 and shāng 殤 (ant. shòu 壽 "long-lived") refer to an early and not just untimely death.
