- Orth: [弒](../../graphs/8/8505eb63-6f4f-4c92-9535-496cf96948f4.md)
  - Pinyin: shì
  - Old Chinese: lʰɯɡs
  - Middle Chinese: ɕɨ

## Definition
Shì 弒 refers to assassination of a ruler, and always with negative connotations (the justified killing of a ruler would still be shā 殺).
