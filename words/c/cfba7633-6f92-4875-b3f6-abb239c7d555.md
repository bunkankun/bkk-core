  - Pinyin: qì
  - Old Chinese: khrɯds
  - Middle Chinese: khi

## Definition
Qì 器 can also refer to the coffin (from Han at least down to Six Dynasties times). Coffins for an emperor can then be called bì qì 秘 器, and those for officials guān qì 棺器.
