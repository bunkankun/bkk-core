## Title
**Men of the Cliffs and Caves**: The Development of the Chinese eremitic Tradition to the End of the Han Dynasty

## Contributors
- Aaat Verwoorn, author

## Publication
Hong Kong: The Chinese University Press, 1990.

## Notes
